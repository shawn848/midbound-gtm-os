# MidBound GTM Roadmap

## Phase 1: Content Engine (Current)

The foundation. Everything needed to produce consistent, on-voice content at cadence.

**Delivered:**
- Voice DNA profiles for Sebastian and Eli
- Product knowledge base (12+ files: what MidBound is, how it works, competitive landscape, integrations snapshot, GEO/AEO references)
- Anti-slop detection adapted for both founders
- Platform playbooks: LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Blog
- 5 content pillars with templates
- Pre-publish checklist and substance requirements
- Live cross-platform content calendar
- **Live Next.js blog at midbound.ai/blog** (rendering `content/blog/{eli,sebastian}/`)
- Initial run of published blog posts by both founders (16+ live or staged)
- Content creation workflow (blog → LinkedIn → X → Reddit)
- **Reddit-to-blog repurpose workflow** (`workflows/reddit-to-blog.md`) — the active daily flow
- **Eli's r/Midbound 6-week content arc** — 12 essay-style posts drafted, Tue/Thu cadence, ground rules + cross-post plan (`content/reddit/eli-arc-1-2026-Q2/`)

**Outcome:** Sebastian and Eli can open Claude Code, ask for a post (fresh or repurposed from Reddit), and get something that sounds like them. The calendar keeps cadence. The checklist catches slop. The blog is live and accepting new posts.

---

## Phase 2: Lead Gen Pipeline (Next)

Connect content to pipeline. The posts drive traffic. MidBound identifies the visitors. The pipeline nurtures them.

**Planned:**
- HubSpot integration: contact properties, lifecycle stages, deal pipeline configured for MidBound's sales motion
- Clay enrichment: visitor data enriched with firmographics, tech stack, intent signals
- Slack notifications: real-time alerts when high-ICP visitors hit key pages
- Sequence templates: follow-up sequences triggered by visitor behavior
- Transcript-to-blog workflow: turn sales calls and founder conversations into content
- UTM tracking framework: tie content pieces to visitor identification

**Outcome:** Content drives visitors. MidBound identifies them. HubSpot tracks them. Sequences engage them. Closed loop.

---

## Phase 3: Full GTM Automation (Later)

Scale what works. Automate the repeatable parts. Keep humans on the creative and strategic work.

**Planned:**
- Signal-based routing: visitor behavior triggers specific content or outreach sequences automatically
- Multi-channel orchestration: coordinate LinkedIn, email, and Slack outreach based on engagement signals
- Content performance feedback loop: track which content pillars drive the most identified visitors and pipeline
- ICP scoring automation: auto-score visitors against ICP criteria and route to appropriate sequences
- Multi-stakeholder playbooks: when multiple people from the same company visit, trigger account-based plays
- Competitive displacement campaigns: targeted content for visitors currently using Snitcher, Factors, or Clearbit

**Outcome:** The GTM engine runs with minimal manual intervention. Founders focus on product and high-value conversations. The system handles distribution, identification, and initial engagement.
