# 7 Best Website Visitor Identification Tools in 2026

97% of your website visitors leave without filling out a form. That's not a guess. That's the industry average for B2B sites.

The question is: do you accept that 97% as lost? Or do you identify them?

Website visitor identification tools exist to close that gap. They take anonymous traffic and turn it into actionable data. But not all tools work the same way. The biggest distinction is between company-level identification (which company visited) and person-level identification (which individual visited).

Here's the math on why that distinction matters. If you're spending $30K per month on ads and driving 10,000 visitors, company-level tools give you 3,000 company names. Your SDR still has to guess who at those companies was browsing. Person-level tools give you names, titles, emails, and LinkedIn profiles. No guessing. Same traffic, wildly different output.

Below is every major tool in the space, ranked by identification depth.

## 1. MidBound (Person-Level)

MidBound identifies the individual person visiting your website. Not the company. The person. Using AI matching against LinkedIn profiles in real-time, MidBound resolves anonymous visitors to named individuals with validated email addresses and ICP scores.

**What you get:** Full name, job title, company, validated email, LinkedIn profile URL, pages visited, time on site, ICP score.

**Key features:**
- AI-powered matching with confidence scoring
- Automated ICP scoring against your criteria
- Multi-stakeholder detection (flags buying committee signals)
- UTM re-engagement (ties identified visitors to campaigns)
- Real-time Slack alerts, HubSpot contact creation, webhooks, sequence enrollment

**Best for:** B2B SaaS teams, demand gen agencies, and ABM teams with 1,000+ US monthly visitors and $3K+ ACV.

**Pricing:** SMB-to-mid-market. 14-day free trial, no credit card required.

**Setup:** Minutes. Lightweight script installation.

**What makes it different:** MidBound is the only tool that combines person-level identification with built-in ICP scoring, multi-stakeholder detection, and campaign attribution. Most tools give you data. MidBound gives you prioritized, actionable intelligence.

## 2. RB2B (Person-Level)

RB2B is a direct competitor to MidBound in the person-level identification category. It resolves anonymous visitors to individual profiles, not just companies.

**What you get:** Name, title, company, email, LinkedIn profile (where available), pages visited.

**Key features:**
- Person-level identification
- Slack notifications
- HubSpot integration

**Best for:** Teams testing person-level identification with a free entry point.

**Pricing:** Freemium model with paid tiers for higher volumes.

**Setup:** Minutes. Script installation.

**Limitations:** No built-in ICP scoring. Limited multi-stakeholder detection. No UTM re-engagement. Webhook and sequence capabilities are less mature than MidBound. Good for proving the concept, but teams that scale typically need more depth.

## 3. Snitcher (Company-Level)

Snitcher is one of the most popular and affordable company-level identification tools. Reverse IP lookup against commercial databases. Simple, reliable, and cheap.

**What you get:** Company name, industry, location, employee count, pages visited, session duration.

**Key features:**
- Company-level identification via IP lookup
- Google Analytics integration
- Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive integrations
- Google Tag Manager support

**Best for:** SMBs on a tight budget who want basic company-level intelligence and are willing to do manual research to find contacts.

**Pricing:** Starting around $39 per month.

**Setup:** Minutes. Lightweight script.

**Limitations:** Company-level only. No individual names, titles, or email addresses. You see "Acme Corp visited" and then manually search LinkedIn to guess who. Works well for office-based traffic but struggles with remote workers whose residential IPs don't map to companies.

## 4. Factors.ai (Account-Level)

Factors.ai combines account identification with multi-touch attribution analytics. It goes beyond simple company identification by layering campaign performance and engagement data.

**What you get:** Account identification, multi-touch attribution reports, account engagement timelines, channel performance analytics.

**Key features:**
- Reverse IP company identification
- Cross-channel attribution modeling
- Account engagement timelines
- Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads integrations

**Best for:** Mid-market marketing teams that need both account identification and attribution analytics. Good for teams that want to understand which channels drive account engagement, not just which companies visit.

**Pricing:** Mid-market. More expensive than Snitcher, less expensive than enterprise ABM.

**Setup:** Hours. Requires integration with ad platforms and CRM for full attribution.

**Limitations:** Account-level identification only. No individual visitor names or contact information. The analytics layer is valuable, but the identification is still "which company" not "which person." Requires more setup than simple identification tools.

## 5. Vector / Clearbit Reveal (Company-Level)

Vector (formerly Clearbit Reveal, now part of HubSpot) provides company-level IP-to-company matching. The broader Clearbit platform enriches known contacts with firmographic and demographic data.

**What you get:** Company name, domain, industry, employee count, estimated revenue, technology stack. For known contacts: email verification, role, seniority.

**Key features:**
- Company-level visitor identification
- Contact enrichment (for known contacts only)
- Native HubSpot integration (owned by HubSpot)
- Technology stack data

**Best for:** HubSpot users who want basic company-level traffic intelligence bundled with their CRM.

**Pricing:** Bundled into HubSpot tiers. Previously standalone from around $99 per month.

**Setup:** Moderate. Best experience within HubSpot ecosystem.

**Limitations:** Company-level only for anonymous visitors. The enrichment features work on contacts already in your CRM, not on unknown visitors. Now tied to the HubSpot ecosystem, which limits flexibility for teams on Salesforce or other CRMs.

## 6. 6sense (Account-Level, Enterprise)

6sense is an enterprise account-based orchestration platform. It combines account identification, multi-source intent data (web, G2, TrustRadius, Bombora), predictive analytics, and advertising into a unified ABM engine.

**What you get:** Account identification, buying stage predictions, intent topics, contact recommendations from their database, orchestration signals.

**Key features:**
- Reverse IP account identification
- Third-party intent data aggregation
- Predictive buying stage scoring
- Account-based advertising
- Sales intelligence dashboards

**Best for:** Enterprise marketing teams with $50K+ budgets, dedicated ops resources, and a need for multi-source intent data at scale.

**Pricing:** Enterprise. Annual contracts typically start at $50K to $100K+.

**Setup:** Weeks to months. Requires dedicated ops, CRM integration, intent configuration, predictive model training.

**Limitations:** Does not identify individual visitors by name. Account-level only. Requires significant investment in both money and implementation time. The platform does far more than identification, but the identification layer is still company-based. MidBound solves the "which specific person visited" problem that 6sense does not address.

## 7. Demandbase (Account-Level, Enterprise)

Demandbase is an enterprise ABM platform providing company identification, account-level advertising, intent data, and sales intelligence. One of the original players in the ABM category.

**What you get:** Account identification, intent topics, account scoring, advertising audiences, contact database, engagement timelines.

**Key features:**
- Company identification with proprietary data
- Account-based advertising
- Intent signal aggregation
- Contact database (from their own data, not visitor identification)
- AI-driven account scoring

**Best for:** Enterprise marketing and sales teams that need a full ABM platform with advertising, intent, and orchestration capabilities.

**Pricing:** Enterprise. Annual contracts in the $50K to $150K+ range.

**Setup:** Multi-week implementation with dedicated ops resources.

**Limitations:** Same as 6sense. Does not identify individual visitors. Account-level identification only. The platform serves enterprise teams with large budgets and complex ABM needs. Not designed for SMB or mid-market teams that need quick, person-level visitor intelligence.

## How to choose the right tool

The decision tree is simpler than vendors want you to believe.

**Do you need to know which person visited, or which company?**

If you need the actual person (name, title, email, LinkedIn), your options are MidBound and RB2B. MidBound if you want ICP scoring, multi-stakeholder detection, and deeper integrations. RB2B if you want a free starting point.

If company-level is enough, Snitcher is the cheapest entry point. Factors adds attribution analytics. Vector works if you're already on HubSpot.

If you're an enterprise team with $50K+ budgets and need full ABM orchestration, 6sense or Demandbase are the established platforms. But even then, layering MidBound on top fills the person-level gap that neither platform addresses.

Accounts don't buy. People do.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between company-level and person-level visitor identification?

Company-level identification tells you which company visited your website using IP-to-company matching. You see "someone from Acme Corp visited your pricing page." Person-level identification tells you the specific individual. You see "Sarah Chen, VP of Marketing at Acme Corp, spent 4 minutes on your pricing page" along with her email and LinkedIn profile.

### Which visitor identification tool is best for B2B SaaS?

For B2B SaaS teams with sales-assisted motions, person-level identification delivers the most value because it enables immediate, contextual outreach. MidBound is designed for this use case, with ICP scoring, real-time Slack alerts, and CRM integration. Teams with smaller budgets can start with Snitcher for company-level intelligence.

### Can I use multiple visitor identification tools together?

Yes. Some teams layer person-level identification (MidBound) on top of enterprise ABM platforms (6sense, Demandbase) to fill the individual visitor gap. MidBound also works alongside Clearbit enrichment since they solve different problems: Clearbit enriches known contacts, MidBound identifies unknown visitors.

### How many website visitors can these tools identify?

Identification rates vary by tool, traffic profile, and geography. Company-level tools typically identify 20 to 40% of traffic at the account level. Person-level tools identify a smaller percentage but with dramatically more actionable data per match. B2B sites with US-heavy traffic see the highest match rates across all tools.

### Do visitor identification tools work for remote workers?

Company-level tools that rely solely on IP-to-company matching struggle with remote workers because residential IPs don't map to companies. Person-level tools like MidBound use AI matching beyond IP addresses, which means they can identify some remote visitors that company-level tools miss entirely.

### Is website visitor identification legal?

Website visitor identification operates in the B2B context where professional identities are publicly available (LinkedIn profiles, company directories). Tools identify business visitors on business websites. Privacy considerations vary by jurisdiction. Teams should consult legal counsel for specific compliance requirements in their markets.
