## Building in Public

Starting a B2B company means making bets on where the market is heading. At MidBound, we believe the future of GTM is **person-level**, not account-level.

### What We've Learned So Far

1. Intent data is only as good as your ability to act on it
2. Person-level identification changes the sales motion entirely
3. The best outbound feels like inbound to the recipient

> Every great B2B company starts by solving a problem the founders experienced themselves.
