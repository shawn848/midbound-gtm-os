# Company-Level ID is Dead. Here's What Replaced It.

Company-level ID is a scam.

Snitcher. Factors. Clearbit. 6sense. Demandbase. All of them.

I don't care how nice the dashboard looks. I don't care how many Salesforce integrations they have. If the output is "someone from Google visited your pricing page," you have nothing.

Google has 300,000 employees.

Which one visited? The intern researching a school project? The procurement lead comparing five vendors? The janitor using the office wifi?

You don't know. And neither does your sales team.

## The lie everyone bought

The pitch was always the same. "We'll tell you which companies are on your website." And B2B teams ate it up because it sounded smart. It sounded like intent data. It sounded like a shortcut.

It's not a shortcut. It's a dead end with better branding.

Here's what actually happens when you run a company-level identification tool:

Your SDR gets a notification. "Microsoft visited your features page." Great. Now what? They go to LinkedIn and search "Microsoft." 228,000 results. They filter by title. Still 4,000 people. They guess. They pick someone who looks right. They send a cold email.

That person has no idea your website exists. Because they weren't the one who visited.

The person who actually visited? A senior analyst three levels below the VP your SDR is spamming. That analyst was comparing you to two competitors. They were ready to champion you internally. But nobody reached out to them because company-level tools couldn't see them.

That deal dies in silence.

I've watched this happen dozens of times.

## The math doesn't work

Let's say you're spending $50K a month on ads. Solid budget. You're driving 10,000 unique visitors to your site. A company-level tool identifies maybe 30% of that traffic at the account level. So you get 3,000 company names.

Now your team has to figure out WHO at those companies was actually browsing. They can't. So they spray emails at anyone with a matching title.

Response rate on those emails? Maybe 2%. Because you're reaching out to people who never visited your site. You're guessing. It's dressed-up cold outbound with extra steps.

That $50K in ad spend just funded a really expensive guessing game.

Speed to lead goes from days to minutes.
