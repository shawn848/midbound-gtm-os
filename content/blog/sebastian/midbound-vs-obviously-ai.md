# MidBound vs Obviously.ai: These Are Not the Same Thing

I keep seeing Obviously.ai come up in searches for "AI website visitor identification."

It doesn't do website visitor identification.

Not even a little bit.

## What Obviously.ai actually does

Obviously.ai is a predictive analytics platform. It lets non-technical teams build machine learning models from structured data. Churn prediction. Lead scoring. Demand forecasting. Revenue modeling.

You feed it a CSV or connect a data source. It builds a model. It makes predictions based on patterns in your existing data.

That's useful. For analytics teams. With existing data.

It does not put a script on your website. It does not identify anonymous visitors. It does not tell you who came to your pricing page.

## Why the confusion exists

Both MidBound and Obviously.ai have "AI" in their positioning. Both serve B2B teams. Both show up in searches for "AI-powered marketing tools."

But they solve entirely different problems.

Obviously.ai answers: "Given the data I already have, what will happen next?"

MidBound answers: "Who is on my website right now that I don't know about?"

One predicts patterns in known data. The other identifies unknown visitors. There is zero overlap.

| Capability | Obviously.ai | MidBound |

| Product category | Predictive analytics | Visitor identification |
| What it needs | Your existing data (CSV, database) | A script on your website |

| Identifies anonymous visitors | No | Yes |

| Sales team actionability | Indirect (through better models) | Direct (contact info for outreach) |

## What you probably need

If you're searching for "AI website visitor identification," you need MidBound. Or maybe RB2B. Or even a company-level tool like Snitcher or Factors if person-level isn't in your budget yet.

You don't need Obviously.ai for this use case. It's a good product for what it does. It just doesn't do visitor identification.

If you need predictive lead scoring on contacts already in your CRM, Obviously.ai might be worth looking at. If you need to know who just visited your pricing page and you want their name, title, email, and LinkedIn profile in your Slack channel within minutes, that's MidBound.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Obviously.ai identify website visitors?

No. Obviously.ai is a predictive analytics platform that builds machine learning models from existing data. It does not place a tracking script on your website and does not identify anonymous visitors. For website visitor identification, tools like MidBound (person-level) or Snitcher (company-level) are the relevant category.

### Why does Obviously.ai appear in visitor identification searches?

Both Obviously.ai and MidBound use AI and serve B2B marketing teams. Search algorithms group them together based on keyword overlap. The products serve fundamentally different functions. Obviously.ai predicts outcomes from existing data. MidBound identifies unknown website visitors in real-time.

### Can I use both MidBound and Obviously.ai together?

Yes, they solve different problems. MidBound identifies anonymous visitors and delivers contact intelligence to your CRM. Obviously.ai could then use that enriched CRM data to build predictive models. They're complementary, not competitive.

### What is the best AI tool for identifying website visitors?

For person-level identification (the name, title, and email of the individual who visited), MidBound and RB2B are the two main options. For company-level identification (which company visited), Snitcher, Factors, and Vector (Clearbit) are the established tools. 6sense and Demandbase provide account-level identification as part of larger enterprise ABM platforms.
