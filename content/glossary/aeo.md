Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is a search strategy focused on structuring content so that AI-powered answer engines -- such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and voice assistants -- select and cite it as a direct response to user queries.

## How It Works

Traditional SEO targets blue-link rankings on a search engine results page. AEO targets a different surface: the synthesized answer that AI systems generate before (or instead of) showing links.

AI answer engines work by pulling information from multiple sources, synthesizing a response, and sometimes citing the original source. To get cited, your content needs to do three things:

1. **Answer the question directly.** The first sentence of a section should deliver a clear, self-contained answer. AI models prioritize content that gets to the point.
2. **Provide structured context.** Use headers, lists, and tables that make it easy for a model to extract the relevant passage. Long, undifferentiated paragraphs get skipped.
3. **Demonstrate authority.** AI models weigh source credibility. Content from recognized domains with consistent topical depth ranks higher in citation selection.

AEO is not a replacement for SEO -- it's an additional layer. Pages that rank well in traditional search often have the authority signals that make them good AEO candidates too.

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

B2B buyers increasingly start their research in AI tools rather than Google. When a VP of Marketing asks ChatGPT "what tools identify website visitors at the person level," the answer it generates becomes the shortlist. If your product is not in that answer, you are not in the conversation.

For B2B marketers, AEO means rethinking content structure: leading with definitions, using FAQ formats, and building topical depth that AI models recognize as authoritative.

## How It Relates to MidBound

MidBound operates in the website visitor identification space -- a category where buyers frequently use AI search to compare tools and understand the difference between company-level and person-level identification. Glossary pages, comparison content, and clear product explanations are all AEO surfaces that help B2B companies get cited when buyers are researching.
