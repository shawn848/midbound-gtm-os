Ahrefs is an SEO toolset best known for its backlink index and analysis capabilities, along with keyword research, rank tracking, content exploration, and site audit features -- used by marketing and SEO teams to improve organic search performance.

## How It Works

Ahrefs operates one of the most active web crawlers in the world (after Google), which powers its backlink database and content index.

Core capabilities:

**Site Explorer.** Enter any domain or URL to see its backlink profile, organic keywords, traffic estimates, and top-performing pages. This is Ahrefs' signature feature -- its backlink data is among the most comprehensive available.

**Keywords Explorer.** Research keywords across Google, YouTube, Amazon, and other search engines. Provides search volume, keyword difficulty, click metrics (what percentage of searches result in clicks), and SERP analysis.

**Content Explorer.** Search for the most linked-to and shared content on any topic. Useful for identifying content formats and angles that earn backlinks and social engagement.

**Rank Tracker.** Monitor keyword rankings over time with desktop and mobile tracking, SERP feature monitoring, and competitor comparison.

**Site Audit.** Crawl your website to identify technical SEO issues, with prioritized recommendations and progress tracking over time.

Ahrefs differentiates from SEMrush primarily in backlink analysis depth and a more focused feature set. Where SEMrush covers a broader range of digital marketing tools (including paid search and social), Ahrefs is more narrowly focused on SEO.

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

Backlinks remain one of the strongest ranking signals in SEO. For B2B companies competing for high-value keywords, Ahrefs helps identify link-building opportunities, analyze competitor backlink strategies, and track the authority of their own domain over time.

The Content Explorer feature is particularly useful for B2B content strategy -- it reveals which topics and formats in your category earn the most links and engagement, informing what to create next.
