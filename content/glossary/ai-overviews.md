Google AI Overviews are AI-generated answer summaries that appear at the top of Google search results, providing a synthesized response to the user's query by pulling information from multiple web sources and citing them inline.

## How It Works

When a user searches a query that Google determines benefits from an AI-generated summary, the AI Overview appears above the traditional organic results. The system:

1. **Identifies relevant sources.** Google's AI pulls from pages it considers authoritative and relevant to the query.
2. **Synthesizes an answer.** Rather than copying text from one source, it combines information from multiple pages into a coherent response.
3. **Cites sources.** Each claim in the overview links back to the source page, giving users a path to dig deeper.

AI Overviews are not triggered for every search. They appear most frequently for informational and research queries -- exactly the type of searches B2B buyers make when evaluating categories and products.

The format is evolving rapidly. Google has expanded AI Overviews from a limited rollout to appearing on a significant percentage of search results, and the citation mechanics continue to change.

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

AI Overviews fundamentally change B2B search behavior in two ways:

First, they push organic results further down the page. A page ranking #1 organically now competes with an AI-generated answer that appears above it. This makes being cited in the AI Overview as important as -- or more important than -- the traditional #1 ranking.

Second, they change the content strategy. Pages optimized for AI Overview citations need clear, definitive answers supported by data and structured formatting. Generic content that repeats what every other page says is unlikely to be selected as a source.

## How It Relates to MidBound

For B2B categories like website visitor identification, AI Overviews are increasingly where buyers get their first exposure to available solutions. Content that clearly explains what person-level identification is, how it differs from company-level tools, and what outcomes it drives has the best chance of being cited -- putting solutions like MidBound in front of buyers at the moment of research.
