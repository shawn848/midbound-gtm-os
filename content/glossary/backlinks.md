A backlink is a hyperlink from one website pointing to a page on another website. Search engines treat backlinks as votes of confidence. When a reputable site links to your content, it signals that your page is trustworthy, relevant, and worth referencing. Backlinks remain one of the strongest ranking factors in both traditional search and AI citation selection.

## Why It Matters

Not all backlinks are equal. Search engines evaluate links based on several factors:

- **Source authority**: A link from a high-authority domain (e.g., a major publication or established industry blog) carries more weight than a link from a low-traffic site.
- **Relevance**: A link from a site in your industry is more valuable than one from an unrelated domain. A B2B SaaS blog linking to your visitor identification guide matters more than a generic directory listing.
- **Anchor text**: The clickable text of the link gives context about what the linked page covers. Descriptive anchor text (e.g., "person-level visitor identification") is more useful than generic text (e.g., "click here").
- **Link placement**: Editorial links within body content carry more authority than footer or sidebar links.

For AI answer engines, backlinks play a specific role in citation selection. When Perplexity, ChatGPT, or Google AI Overviews synthesize answers, they prioritize sources that other authoritative sites already reference. A page with 50 quality backlinks from relevant domains is more likely to be cited than a similar page with zero external references.

B2B companies build backlinks through original research, data-driven content, expert commentary in industry publications, and creating reference content (like glossaries and comparison pages) that others naturally link to.

## How It Relates to MidBound

Backlinks drive referral traffic from external sites, and that traffic is often high-intent. When an industry blog links to your comparison page or glossary term, the visitors clicking through are actively researching a topic related to your product. MidBound identifies those referral visitors at the person level, showing you not just that someone came from a backlink but exactly who they are, what company they work for, and which pages they visited. This turns passive link-building into an active pipeline source.
