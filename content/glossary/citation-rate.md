Citation rate is a Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) metric that measures how frequently a specific source or domain is cited by AI-powered search engines when generating answers to queries within a given topic area.

## How It Works

When generative search engines like Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, or ChatGPT produce answers, they select and cite sources from across the web. Citation rate tracks how often your content is selected as one of those sources.

Measuring citation rate involves:

1. **Tracking target queries.** Identify the set of queries relevant to your business (e.g., "what is website visitor identification" or "best B2B lead generation tools").
2. **Monitoring AI responses.** Check which sources the generative engine cites for each query. This can be done manually or through emerging GEO monitoring tools.
3. **Calculating frequency.** Citation rate = (number of queries where your domain is cited) / (total queries monitored) for a given topic cluster.

Unlike traditional SEO metrics (rankings, traffic, click-through rate), citation rate measures visibility in a context where the user may never click through to your site. The citation itself is the visibility -- your brand and content appear in the answer the user reads.

Factors that increase citation rate include: using specific data and statistics, demonstrating topical authority across multiple related pages, providing unique insights not available on competing pages, and structuring content for easy extraction by AI models.

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

As more B2B research starts in generative search tools, citation rate becomes a leading indicator of brand visibility. A company with a high citation rate in their category is consistently appearing in the answers buyers read during evaluation. A company with a zero citation rate is invisible in this channel -- regardless of how well they rank in traditional search.

For B2B content teams, tracking citation rate reveals whether their content strategy is working in the AI search era or whether they are optimizing for a distribution channel (traditional blue links) that is shrinking in influence.

## How It Relates to MidBound

In the visitor identification category, citation rate determines which solutions show up when buyers ask AI tools to compare options. B2B companies that build authoritative, data-rich content around their category -- like comprehensive glossaries and comparison pages -- improve their citation rate and stay visible as search behavior shifts.
