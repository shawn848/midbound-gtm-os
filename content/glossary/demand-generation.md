Demand generation is the set of marketing activities that create awareness, educate potential buyers, and build interest in your product before those buyers actively enter a sales process. Unlike lead generation, which focuses on capturing contact information through forms and gated content, demand generation focuses on making buyers want to engage in the first place.

## How It Works

Demand generation operates across the full buyer journey:

- **Awareness**: Content marketing, social media, SEO, and paid media that introduce your brand and category to buyers who do not yet know they have a problem you solve.
- **Education**: Blog posts, glossary content, webinars, and podcasts that help buyers understand the problem space and evaluate approaches. This is where most B2B demand gen effort concentrates.
- **Consideration**: Comparison content, case studies, and product-led content that positions your solution for buyers who are actively evaluating options.

The core tension in B2B demand gen is measurement. Demand gen creates value by influencing buyers long before they fill out a form, but traditional attribution models only credit the form fill. This creates a gap: your content might be driving significant research activity and shaping buying decisions, but your analytics only show anonymous traffic.

Modern demand gen stacks address this with several layers:

1. **Content and distribution** to reach the right audience.
2. **SEO and AEO** to capture organic and AI-referred traffic.
3. **Intent data** to identify accounts showing research behavior.
4. **Visitor identification** to connect anonymous traffic to real people.
5. **CRM and sequences** to route identified buyers to sales.

The most effective demand gen programs treat visitor identification as a core infrastructure layer, not an add-on. It bridges the gap between generating demand and capturing it.

## How It Relates to MidBound

MidBound solves demand gen's biggest measurement problem: the anonymous visitor gap. Most B2B websites convert only 2-5% of visitors through forms. The other 95-98% consume your demand gen content and leave without identifying themselves. MidBound identifies those visitors at the person level, showing you exactly who is reading your blog posts, checking your pricing page, and comparing you to competitors. This means your demand gen efforts produce identifiable pipeline, not just traffic numbers. Your team can see which content attracts ICP-fit buyers and follow up with the specific people who engaged, closing the loop between demand creation and revenue.
