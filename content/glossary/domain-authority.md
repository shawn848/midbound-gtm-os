Domain Authority (DA) is a metric developed by Moz that predicts how well a website will rank in search engine results on a scale from 1 to 100. Ahrefs has a similar metric called Domain Rating (DR). While neither metric is used directly by Google's algorithm, both correlate strongly with search rankings and serve as useful benchmarks for evaluating a site's competitive strength.

## How It Works

Domain authority is calculated based on several factors:

- **Backlink profile**: The number and quality of external sites linking to your domain. More links from high-authority, relevant sites increase your score.
- **Referring domains**: The diversity of unique domains linking to you matters more than raw link count. 100 links from 100 different sites outweighs 100 links from one site.
- **Link growth trajectory**: Steady, organic link growth signals legitimacy. Sudden spikes can indicate manipulation and may not improve your score.
- **Root domain age and history**: Established domains with consistent content publishing tend to score higher over time.

Moz DA and Ahrefs DR use different calculation methodologies, so the scores are not interchangeable. A site might have a Moz DA of 45 and an Ahrefs DR of 52. The relative ranking within each system is what matters, not the absolute number.

For AI answer engines, domain authority plays an indirect but significant role. AI models like those powering ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews tend to cite sources from domains that are already well-established and frequently referenced. A high-authority B2B domain is more likely to be selected as a citation source than a newer site with thin content, even if the newer site's page is technically accurate.

Building domain authority is a long-term effort. It requires consistent content publishing, earning editorial backlinks, and developing topical depth in your category.

## How It Relates to MidBound

Domain authority determines how much organic and AI-referred traffic your site can capture. Higher authority means more visitors across more search queries. MidBound adds the identification layer on top of that traffic. Instead of seeing anonymous sessions in Google Analytics, MidBound reveals the specific people visiting your site, their job titles, companies, and which pages they viewed. For B2B teams investing in domain authority through content and link-building, MidBound ensures that growing traffic translates into identifiable pipeline rather than vanity metrics.
