E-E-A-T stands for Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness -- a framework Google uses in its Search Quality Rater Guidelines to evaluate the quality and credibility of web content and its creators.

## How It Works

E-E-A-T is not a direct ranking algorithm. It is a set of principles that Google's human quality raters use to evaluate search results, which in turn inform how Google's algorithms are trained and updated.

**Experience** (added in 2022) evaluates whether the content creator has first-hand experience with the topic. A founder writing about building a startup has more experience signal than a content mill producing generic advice.

**Expertise** assesses the creator's knowledge depth. For technical topics -- like how website visitor identification works -- content from practitioners with demonstrated domain knowledge scores higher than surface-level summaries.

**Authoritativeness** looks at the creator's and site's reputation in the field. This includes backlinks from other authoritative sources, brand mentions, industry recognition, and consistent topical coverage.

**Trustworthiness** is the foundation. It evaluates whether the site and content are accurate, honest, and safe. This includes factual accuracy, transparent authorship, clear business information, and secure site infrastructure (HTTPS).

For B2B content, E-E-A-T signals are strongest when content is authored by named individuals with relevant roles, published on sites with clear business identity, and supported by original data or first-hand case studies.

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

In B2B markets, buyers are making high-stakes purchasing decisions. Google prioritizes content from sources that demonstrate real expertise and authority in those domains. A blog post about visitor identification written by a company that actually builds visitor identification software carries more E-E-A-T weight than a generic marketing blog covering the same topic.

B2B teams can strengthen E-E-A-T by attributing content to named authors with real titles, linking to original research, and building consistent topical depth rather than publishing one-off posts on unrelated subjects.

## How It Relates to MidBound

MidBound's content strategy benefits from strong E-E-A-T signals -- content authored by founders who have direct experience building and selling visitor identification technology. For any B2B company, the combination of authentic expertise and consistent topic coverage is what separates content that ranks from content that gets buried.
