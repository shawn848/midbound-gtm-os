First-party data is information collected directly from your audience through channels you own and operate. This includes website behavior, form submissions, email engagement, product usage, and purchase history. It stands in contrast to third-party data, which is aggregated from external sources and sold through data brokers or advertising platforms.

## Why It Matters

The deprecation of third-party cookies across major browsers has made first-party data the foundation of modern B2B marketing. Safari and Firefox already block third-party cookies by default. Chrome has restricted their use significantly. This means the advertising and retargeting strategies that relied on cross-site tracking are losing effectiveness every quarter.

First-party data is more valuable for several reasons:

- **Accuracy**: You collected it directly, so it reflects actual behavior on your properties rather than inferred or modeled data.
- **Compliance**: First-party data collection, when done with proper consent mechanisms, aligns with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations more naturally than third-party data.
- **Durability**: It does not depend on browser cookie policies or platform decisions. Your CRM data, website analytics, and visitor identification data are yours.
- **Relevance**: Someone who visited your pricing page three times is a stronger signal than someone who appeared in a purchased intent data list.

Website visitor identification is a form of first-party data collection. When a tool identifies visitors on your own website, that data originates from your property and your direct audience interaction. It is not scraped from third-party sources or inferred from bidstream data.

## How It Relates to MidBound

MidBound generates first-party data by identifying the specific people visiting your website. Instead of relying on third-party intent signals or purchased contact lists, MidBound gives you person-level data from your own traffic. You see the name, title, company, and email of visitors who are already engaging with your content. This is first-party data at its most actionable: real people demonstrating real interest on your own property. As third-party cookies disappear, this direct identification approach becomes the most reliable way to know who is in your pipeline before they fill out a form.
