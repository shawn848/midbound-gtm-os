Google Search Console (GSC) is a free tool from Google that allows website owners to monitor, maintain, and troubleshoot their site's presence in Google Search results -- providing data on search queries, click-through rates, indexing status, and technical issues.

## How It Works

GSC provides data directly from Google about how your site appears in search results:

**Search performance reports** show which queries drive impressions and clicks to your site, along with average position and click-through rate (CTR) for each query. This is the only source of actual search query data -- GA4 shows traffic volumes but not the specific queries users searched.

**Index coverage** reports which of your pages Google has indexed, which it has excluded, and why. This surfaces crawl errors, redirect issues, and pages blocked by robots.txt or noindex tags.

**Core Web Vitals** reports show real-user performance data for your pages, flagging URLs that need improvement for LCP, FID/INP, and CLS.

**URL inspection** lets you check how Google sees a specific URL -- whether it is indexed, when it was last crawled, and whether there are any issues with mobile usability or structured data.

**Sitemaps** allow you to submit your XML sitemap so Google can discover and crawl your pages more efficiently.

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

GSC is the ground truth for SEO performance. While third-party tools like SEMrush and Ahrefs estimate rankings, GSC provides actual data from Google: exactly which queries your pages appear for, how many impressions and clicks you received, and what your average position is.

For B2B companies, GSC reveals which buyer-intent queries are driving traffic and where ranking improvements would have the most impact on pipeline.

## How It Relates to MidBound

GSC shows B2B teams which search queries bring visitors to their site. MidBound shows who those visitors are. Combining the two gives a complete picture: which search terms attracted which identified visitors, connecting organic search performance directly to pipeline-quality leads.
