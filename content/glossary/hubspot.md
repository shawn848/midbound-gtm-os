HubSpot CRM is a cloud-based customer relationship management platform that unifies marketing automation, sales pipeline management, and customer service tools into a single system -- widely used by B2B teams as their operational hub for managing contacts, deals, and campaigns.

## How It Works

HubSpot organizes customer data around **contacts** (people), **companies** (accounts), and **deals** (opportunities). Marketing teams use it for email campaigns, landing pages, and lead scoring. Sales teams use it for pipeline tracking, sequences, and meeting scheduling. The platform's strength is that all of these functions share a single database, so a contact's full history is visible across teams.

For B2B companies running account-based strategies, HubSpot serves as the system of record for every interaction -- from first website visit to closed deal.

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

HubSpot's value increases when it receives high-quality data. The challenge is that most CRM records start with a form fill or a manual entry. Without visitor identification, the contacts in HubSpot represent only the 3% of website visitors who raised their hand.

When visitor identification tools sync directly to HubSpot, the CRM starts capturing the other 97%. Each identified visitor becomes a contact with full context: their name, title, company, email, pages visited, and time on site.

## How It Relates to MidBound

MidBound integrates directly with HubSpot. When a visitor is identified, their profile is synced to HubSpot as a new contact or matched to an existing one. The contact record includes behavioral data -- which pages they visited, how long they stayed, and whether they match your ICP. Sales teams see these contacts in their pipeline without any manual data entry.
