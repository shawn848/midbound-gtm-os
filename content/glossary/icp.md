An Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) is a detailed description of the type of company -- and the specific roles within that company -- that gets the most value from your product, has the shortest sales cycle, and delivers the highest lifetime value.

## How It Works

An ICP is built from data on your best existing customers. It defines the characteristics that predict whether a prospect will convert, adopt successfully, and retain:

**Company-level criteria:**
- Industry or vertical
- Company size (employees or revenue)
- Geography
- Technology stack
- Business model (B2B vs. B2C, SaaS vs. services)
- Growth stage (startup, scale-up, enterprise)

**Person-level criteria:**
- Job title and seniority
- Department (marketing, sales, ops, engineering)
- Decision-making authority (buyer, influencer, end user)
- Pain points and priorities

The ICP is not the same as a buyer persona. The ICP defines the attributes of the ideal company and role. The buyer persona describes the behavioral patterns, motivations, and objections of the person in that role. ICP is about fit; persona is about psychology.

B2B teams use their ICP to prioritize leads, focus ABM target lists, qualify inbound inquiries, and allocate resources. A lead that matches your ICP criteria gets fast, focused attention. A lead outside your ICP gets a different treatment or is deprioritized.

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

Without a well-defined ICP, sales and marketing teams waste time on prospects who will never close or who will churn quickly after closing. A tight ICP focuses effort on the highest-probability, highest-value opportunities.

ICP also informs content strategy, ad targeting, and messaging. When you know exactly who you are trying to reach, every piece of marketing becomes more targeted and effective.

## How It Relates to MidBound

MidBound uses ICP criteria to score identified website visitors. When a visitor is identified, MidBound compares their profile (title, seniority, company size, industry) against the customer's ICP definition and assigns a fit score. High-ICP visitors surface first and can trigger immediate Slack alerts or automated outreach sequences. This means sales reps spend their time on the visitors most likely to become customers.
