Intent data is a category of behavioral signals that indicate a company or individual is actively researching, evaluating, or showing interest in solutions within a specific product category -- helping B2B sales and marketing teams identify and prioritize prospects who are likely in an active buying cycle.

## How It Works

Intent data comes from three primary sources:

**First-party intent data** is collected from your own digital properties. Website visits, content downloads, pricing page views, demo requests, and email engagement are all first-party intent signals. This is the highest-fidelity data because it reflects direct interaction with your brand.

**Second-party intent data** comes from a partner's platform -- for example, review sites like G2 that share data about which companies are researching your category.

**Third-party intent data** is aggregated from across the web by vendors (like Bombora, 6sense, and Demandbase) who track content consumption patterns across thousands of websites. When a company shows elevated research activity around keywords related to your category, it is flagged as showing intent.

Each source has trade-offs. First-party data is high quality but low volume (you only see people who already found you). Third-party data is broader but noisier (the signals are less directly tied to your product).

Intent signals are typically scored and prioritized. A single blog post visit is a weak signal. A pricing page visit followed by a demo page visit followed by a case study download is a strong signal.

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

Timing matters in B2B sales. Reaching out to a prospect who is actively evaluating solutions converts at significantly higher rates than cold outreach to someone who is not in a buying cycle. Intent data helps teams identify the right timing and prioritize outreach accordingly.

Intent data also helps ABM teams decide which accounts to focus on this quarter. Instead of spreading effort equally across all target accounts, teams can concentrate on the ones showing active buying signals.

## How It Relates to MidBound

MidBound provides the strongest form of first-party intent data: identified visitors on your own website. When someone visits your pricing page, that is a high-intent signal. When MidBound identifies that visitor as a Director of Demand Gen at a target account, the signal becomes immediately actionable. Unlike third-party intent data, which tells you a company is researching a category somewhere on the web, MidBound tells you a specific person is researching your product on your website right now.
