Net Revenue Retention (NRR) is the percentage of recurring revenue retained from existing customers over a given period, accounting for expansions, contractions, and churn -- measuring whether your customer base is growing or shrinking independent of new sales.

## How It Works

NRR is calculated as:

**NRR = (Starting MRR + Expansion - Contraction - Churn) / Starting MRR x 100**

An NRR of 100% means you retained every dollar. Above 100% means existing customers pay more over time. Below 100% means your customer base is leaking revenue.

Top-performing B2B SaaS companies target NRR above 120%. That means even without a single new customer, revenue grows 20% annually from the existing base.

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

NRR reflects product-market fit, customer satisfaction, and growth efficiency simultaneously. High NRR means your product becomes more valuable over time. Low NRR means you are constantly replacing revenue that is walking out the door.

A company with 90% NRR and 100 new customers is running to stand still. A company with 130% NRR and 50 new customers is growing faster.
