Person-Based Marketing (PBM) is the evolution of Account-Based Marketing that shifts focus from targeting companies to targeting the specific individuals within those companies who influence or make purchasing decisions.

## How It Works

ABM treats each account as a single entity. PBM goes further by recognizing that purchasing decisions are made by people -- and different people at the same account have different roles, priorities, and concerns.

PBM operates by:

1. **Identifying individuals.** Instead of just targeting "Acme Corp," PBM identifies the specific people at Acme Corp who are involved in purchasing decisions -- the VP evaluating solutions, the director managing the implementation, the end user who will adopt the tool.
2. **Personalizing by persona.** Each individual receives messaging relevant to their role. The VP gets ROI-focused content. The technical lead gets integration details. The end user gets workflow examples.
3. **Tracking individual engagement.** PBM tracks which specific people are engaging with your content, visiting your site, and interacting with your brand -- not just which accounts.
4. **Timing outreach to individuals.** When a specific high-value individual shows engagement signals (visiting the pricing page, downloading a whitepaper), outreach is triggered immediately to that person.

PBM requires person-level data that ABM does not: you need to know who is visiting, not just which company is visiting. This is why person-level identification technology is the enabling infrastructure for PBM.

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

Accounts do not sign contracts. People do. The shift from ABM to PBM reflects a fundamental truth about B2B sales: every deal is won or lost based on conversations with specific individuals. Knowing which individuals are engaged, what they care about, and when they are active gives sales teams a significant advantage.

PBM also improves marketing efficiency. Instead of running broad account-level campaigns that reach everyone at a target company (including people who have no role in the buying decision), PBM focuses resources on the individuals who matter.
