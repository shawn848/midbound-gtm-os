Person-level identification is the practice of resolving anonymous website visitors to specific individuals -- providing their name, job title, company, validated email address, and LinkedIn profile -- rather than only identifying the company they work for.

## How It Works

Person-level identification uses AI matching to connect a website visitor's session to a real individual in a proprietary dataset. The process works differently from company-level tools, which rely primarily on reverse IP lookup.

The identification flow:

1. **Session capture.** A script on the website records the visitor's session data -- pages viewed, time spent, referral source, UTM parameters.
2. **AI matching.** The identification engine processes the visitor signal and matches it against a dataset of professional profiles, resolving to a specific LinkedIn profile with a confidence score.
3. **Profile enrichment.** The matched individual's data is enriched with full name, current job title, company, validated email address, and LinkedIn URL.
4. **ICP scoring.** The identified visitor is scored against the customer's Ideal Customer Profile criteria -- title, seniority, company size, industry -- so high-value visitors surface first.
5. **Real-time delivery.** Results are pushed to Slack, HubSpot, webhooks, or sequences immediately.

The key distinction: company-level tools tell you "someone from Acme Corp visited." Person-level tools tell you "Sarah Chen, VP of Demand Gen at Acme Corp, spent 4 minutes on your pricing page."

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

Accounts do not buy products. People do. When a sales rep knows the exact person who visited, they can reach out with relevant context the same day. The outreach is warm (the person was already on the site), personalized (the rep knows their role and what they looked at), and timely (same-day follow-up while the product is top of mind).

Company-level identification forces reps to guess who visited and reach out cold to people who may not have been the actual visitor. Person-level identification removes the guesswork entirely.
