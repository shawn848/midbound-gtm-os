Retargeting is a marketing strategy that serves ads to people who have previously visited your website but did not convert. Traditional retargeting relies on browser cookies to track visitors across the web and display ads to them on other sites. In B2B, retargeting is commonly used through platforms like Google Display Network, LinkedIn, and Meta to stay visible during long sales cycles.

## How It Works

Traditional cookie-based retargeting follows a standard flow:

1. A visitor lands on your website. A tracking pixel drops a cookie in their browser.
2. When that visitor browses other sites in the ad network, the cookie identifies them as a past visitor.
3. Your retargeting campaign serves them an ad, often customized based on which pages they viewed.

This approach has three growing problems in B2B:

- **Cookie deprecation**: Safari and Firefox block third-party cookies. Chrome has imposed significant restrictions. The cookie pool shrinks every quarter.
- **Low match rates**: Even when cookies work, B2B retargeting audiences are small. A site with 5,000 monthly visitors might only match 1,500-2,000 for retargeting after cookie loss and cross-device gaps.
- **Anonymous audiences**: Cookie-based retargeting shows ads to browser profiles, not people. You cannot prioritize your ICP or filter out irrelevant visitors because you do not know who they are.

Person-level retargeting takes a different approach. Instead of relying on cookies, it starts with identifying who visited your site, then uses that identity data to build targeted audiences on advertising platforms or trigger direct outreach through email and LinkedIn.

## How It Relates to MidBound

MidBound replaces the anonymous cookie with a known identity. When a visitor lands on your site, MidBound identifies them at the person level, providing their name, title, company, and validated email. This enables a fundamentally different retargeting approach. Instead of hoping a cookie follows an anonymous browser across the web, you can upload identified visitors to LinkedIn Matched Audiences for targeted ads, add them to email sequences, or trigger a Slack notification for immediate sales outreach. The retargeting is based on who the person actually is and whether they match your ICP, not on whether a cookie happened to survive.
