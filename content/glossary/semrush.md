SEMrush is an all-in-one digital marketing platform that provides tools for keyword research, competitive analysis, rank tracking, site audits, backlink analysis, and content optimization -- used by marketing teams to plan and execute SEO and paid search strategies.

## How It Works

SEMrush maintains one of the largest keyword and domain databases in the industry, built by crawling search results and tracking ranking positions across millions of keywords.

Core capabilities:

**Keyword research.** Find keywords your target audience is searching for, along with search volume, difficulty scores, SERP features, and related terms. SEMrush's Keyword Magic Tool generates keyword clusters and identifies opportunities.

**Competitive analysis.** Enter a competitor's domain and see their top-ranking keywords, estimated organic traffic, backlink profile, and paid search campaigns. This reveals where competitors are strong and where gaps exist.

**Rank tracking.** Monitor your rankings for target keywords over time, across different locations and devices. Track progress against competitors on the same keywords.

**Site audit.** Crawl your website to identify technical SEO issues -- broken links, missing meta tags, slow pages, crawlability problems -- with prioritized recommendations.

**Content optimization.** SEMrush's SEO Writing Assistant and Content Template tools analyze top-ranking content for a target keyword and provide recommendations on length, readability, semantically related terms, and structure.

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

For B2B companies, SEMrush is a strategic planning tool. It answers questions like: Which keywords should we target? What are competitors ranking for that we are not? Where should we invest in content? How is our SEO performance trending?

The competitive intelligence is especially valuable in B2B categories where a small number of companies compete for the same high-intent keywords.

## How It Relates to MidBound

SEMrush helps B2B teams drive organic traffic by identifying the right keywords to target. MidBound identifies who that organic traffic actually is. SEMrush optimizes the top of the funnel (getting visitors to the site); MidBound captures the middle (identifying who visited and routing them to sales).
