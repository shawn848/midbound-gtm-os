Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the practice of improving a website's visibility in organic (non-paid) search engine results by optimizing content quality, technical performance, and authority signals so that search engines rank the site higher for relevant queries.

## How It Works

SEO operates across three pillars:

**Content optimization** involves creating pages that match what users are searching for. This means targeting specific keywords, answering questions comprehensively, and structuring content so search engines understand what each page is about. For B2B companies, this typically means building out product pages, comparison content, glossary terms, and educational blog posts.

**Technical SEO** ensures search engines can crawl, index, and render your site efficiently. This includes site speed (Core Web Vitals), mobile responsiveness, clean URL structures, proper use of canonical tags, structured data markup, and XML sitemaps.

**Authority building** involves earning backlinks, brand mentions, and topical authority signals that tell search engines your site is a trusted source. In B2B, this often comes from industry publications, partner integrations, and original research.

Modern SEO also overlaps with AEO and GEO -- the same content principles that drive organic rankings also influence whether AI search engines cite your pages.

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

For B2B companies, organic search is often the largest source of high-intent website traffic. A prospect searching "website visitor identification tools" or "how to identify anonymous website visitors" is actively evaluating solutions. Ranking for these queries puts your product in front of buyers at the moment of intent -- without paying per click.

SEO compounds over time. A well-optimized page continues driving qualified traffic months or years after publication, making it one of the highest-ROI marketing channels for B2B teams with longer sales cycles.
