Speed to lead is the metric that measures the elapsed time between a prospect's first expression of interest and the sales team's first meaningful response -- widely documented as one of the strongest predictors of conversion in B2B sales.

## How It Works

The concept is straightforward: the faster you respond to a prospect showing interest, the more likely they are to convert.

Research on speed to lead has consistently shown:

- Responding within 5 minutes of a prospect's inquiry makes you **21x more likely** to qualify the lead compared to responding after 30 minutes.
- The odds of qualifying a lead drop by **10x** if you wait longer than the first 5 minutes.
- Most B2B companies respond in 42+ hours on average -- well past the window where response timing has a meaningful impact.

The traditional speed-to-lead workflow focuses on form submissions and demo requests: a prospect fills out a form, the system routes it to a rep, and the rep responds. The metric measures the gap between form submission and first contact.

But there is a larger opportunity: visitors who show interest by browsing your site but never fill out a form. These visitors are expressing intent (they are on your pricing page, reading your case studies) but do not generate a lead for sales to follow up on. Without identification, these visitors are invisible, and their speed to lead is effectively infinite.

## Why It Matters for B2B Teams

In competitive B2B markets, the first company to respond often wins the deal. When a prospect is evaluating multiple solutions, the vendor who reaches out while the prospect is still in research mode has a significant advantage. The ones who follow up days later are already behind.

Speed to lead is especially critical for high-ACV deals where the sales cycle is long but the evaluation window is short. The prospect may spend a few hours researching solutions. The vendor who reaches them that same day shapes the conversation.
