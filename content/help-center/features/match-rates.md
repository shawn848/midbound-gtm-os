# How Match Rates Work (And What Affects Them)

Match rate is the percentage of your website visitors that MidBound identifies at the person level. It is the most common question new users have, and the honest answer is: it depends on your traffic.

## Realistic Expectations

Person-level identification rates typically range from **5-20%** of total website visitors. This varies based on your traffic profile.

That range is honest. Anyone claiming 40%+ person-level identification rates is either measuring something different (company-level, not person-level) or inflating numbers.

Even at 5-10%, the math works. If your site gets 5,000 monthly visitors and MidBound identifies 10%, that is 500 people with names, titles, emails, and LinkedIn profiles that you did not have before. If 20% of those match your ICP, that is 100 warm leads per month from traffic you were already paying for.

## What Affects Match Rates

### Traffic geography

US-based traffic matches at the highest rates. The identification dataset has the strongest coverage in the United States. International traffic, particularly from regions with lower LinkedIn adoption, matches at lower rates.

### Business vs. consumer visitors

B2B traffic matches better than consumer traffic. If your site attracts a mix of business professionals and general consumers, your overall rate will be lower. Pages that attract business-intent visitors (pricing, case studies, product pages) tend to have higher match rates than blog posts that attract a broad audience.

### VPN and private browsing

Visitors using VPNs or incognito/private browsing windows are significantly harder to identify. Industries where VPN usage is common (security, IT) may see lower rates.

### Bot traffic

Bots do not match. If a significant portion of your traffic is bots (common with certain ad networks or social platforms), your match rate will appear lower. Filter bot traffic in your analytics to get a more accurate picture.

### Office vs. remote workers

Traffic from corporate networks and office environments tends to match at higher rates than traffic from residential networks.

## How to Optimize for Higher Rates

1. **Focus paid campaigns on US traffic.** If you are running LinkedIn or Google ads, target the US for the highest identification rates.
2. **Use UTM-tagged campaigns.** Campaign-driven traffic from professional platforms (LinkedIn, industry newsletters) performs well.
3. **Drive traffic to business-intent pages.** Pricing, case studies, and product pages attract visitors who are more identifiable than casual blog readers.
4. **Filter bot traffic.** Use your analytics platform to exclude known bots so your match rate reflects real visitor performance.
5. **Check your traffic sources.** Organic search and LinkedIn referrals tend to produce higher match rates than display ads or social media shares.

## Tracking Your Match Rate

Your MidBound dashboard shows your match rate over time. Check it weekly rather than daily, since daily fluctuations are normal. Look at the trend over 30 days for a stable picture.
