Person-Based Marketing is exactly what ABM always wanted to be, but couldn't.

ABM aligned sales and marketing around the same accounts. PBM aligns them around the same people.

Think about it like screen resolution.

Company-level identification is 480p. You see shapes. "Someone from Google visited." Which of the 300K employees? Good luck.

Account-level ABM is 720p. Better targeting. You know which logos to focus on. But you're still guessing at the individual level.

Person-Based Marketing is 4K. You see the individual. Name, role, company, what they looked at, how long they stayed, their email, their LinkedIn.

Here's what PBM actually does differently:

→ ICP pinpointing at the individual level, not just account level
→ Multi-stakeholder detection in real time (three people from the same account visiting in one week = buying committee forming)
→ UTM campaign attribution to specific humans, not just click metrics

These aren't wish-list features. This exists today. MidBound does all of this right now.

Your CMO asks "what did our $40K LinkedIn campaign produce?" Instead of "247 clicks and 8 form fills," you can say "we identified 89 ICP-matched visitors including 12 at target accounts."

ABM was limited by what was technically possible when it was defined. That limitation no longer exists.

The category is forming. Which side of it will you be on?
