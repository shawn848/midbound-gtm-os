Imagine you threw a party and 97% of the people who showed up didn't even say hi to you.

That's your website.

They walked through your front door. Checked out the kitchen (your pricing page). Flipped through your family photos (case studies). Opened the fridge to see what you're working with (features page). Someone even spent five minutes reading your bookshelf (documentation).

Then they left. Without a word.

You're standing in your own house with no idea who was there.

Now imagine you had a friend at the party who knew everyone. Not in a weird way. In a "I know that person because they have a public professional profile" way.

"That's David Rodriguez, Director of Growth at Microsoft. He just spent 3 minutes checking out your kitchen. Here's his email and LinkedIn."

You'd walk over and have a conversation. Not a cold pitch. A warm one. He was already interested enough to show up.

That's what person-level website visitor identification does.

97% of your visitors leave without converting. They're not lost leads. They're identified buyers you never reached out to.

If you're spending real money on ads driving traffic, this is the gap in your funnel you probably haven't measured.

What would your team do differently if they could see exactly who was visiting every day?
