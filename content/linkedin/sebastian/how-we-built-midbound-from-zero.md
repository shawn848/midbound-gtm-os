This is how we started MidBound. true story.

We raised $70K from friends and family. Not VCs. People who trusted us more than our pitch deck justified.

Built an auto-prospecting tool first. Thought we were early.

We weren't. AISDR got commoditized overnight. Every second startup on Product Hunt was launching the same thing.

Killed it. Started over.

I cold called 10 founders a day. Every day. Not warm intros. Cold.

The answer kept coming back to the same problem.

"We spend all this money driving traffic and we have no idea who's actually visiting."

Over and over. Different companies. Same pain.

So we built it. MeetVisitors became MidBound. Person-level website visitor identification.

Six figures in revenue. Under five months. $0 in customer acquisition cost. Team of three.

I'm Venezuelan. Moved to Israel. Served in a combat unit. Then startups.

Comfort didn't make me. Pressure did.

The period between killing product one and finding MidBound was the lowest point. No direction. Friends and family money running out.

But I'd been in worse spots.

Every founder I know who made it through has a version of this story. What's yours?
