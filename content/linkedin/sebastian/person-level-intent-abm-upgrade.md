Marketers spending $50K/month on ads with zero clue who visited.

That's not a budget problem. It's a visibility problem.

You drive 10,000 visitors to your site. Company-level tools identify maybe 30% at the account level. So you get 3,000 company names.

Your team still has to guess WHO at those companies was browsing.

Response rate on those emails? Maybe 2%. Because you're reaching out to people who never visited your site.

That $50K just funded a really expensive guessing game.

Person-level intent changes this completely.

Someone hits your pricing page. You get a Slack notification. "Sarah Kim, Head of Demand Gen at Notion, 4 minutes on pricing. Here's her email and LinkedIn."

Your SDR reaches out while Sarah is still thinking about you. Not next week. Right now.

Speed to lead drops from days to minutes. You're always the first vendor to reach out.

Multi-stakeholder detection becomes automatic. Three people from the same account visiting in the same week? That's a buying committee forming in real time.

ICP matching happens at the individual level. Not "this company matches." This person matches and is on your pricing page right now.

ABM gave you the framework. Person-level intent gives you the actual humans.

If your site gets 1,000+ US visitors a month, you're leaving deals on the table every day. Not theoretically. Actually.
