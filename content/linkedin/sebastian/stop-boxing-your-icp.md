I see companies box their ICP so tight they kill their own pipeline.

Real story. $50M ARR company. ICP locked down to VP+ only. 40-point targeting checklist.

Pipeline was drying up.

During a POC with us, MidBound surfaced a visitor on their pricing page. An analyst. Not VP. Not director. An analyst.

Their SDR team would have never prospected this person.

But the analyst was on the pricing page. For three minutes. Comparing features. Reading case studies.

The AE reached out. Turns out the analyst was leading the entire evaluation internally. The VP everyone was chasing? He'd delegated the buying process.

$30K deal. Closed.

From someone who didn't pass the title filter.

Titles lie. An analyst running an evaluation committee has more influence over a deal than the executive who eventually rubber-stamps it.

Your ICP should be a guide, not a gate.

There's a difference between "we target VP+ at mid-market SaaS" and "we ONLY talk to VP+ at mid-market SaaS."

The first is a strategy. The second is a blindfold.

Who else has seen a deal close from someone completely outside the typical ICP?
