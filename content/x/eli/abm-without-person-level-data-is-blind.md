Your SDR finds 5 contacts at a target account. Runs sequences for 2 weeks. Meanwhile someone from that account visited your pricing page 3 times. Your tools don't tell you who. Organized guessing.

**Tweet 2:**
Real story: customer had been working a target account for months. Sequences on VP of Sales and Director of Marketing. During a POC, we surfaced an analyst on their pricing page. Analyst was leading the evaluation.

**Tweet 3:**
$30K closed deal. From an analyst their team would never have prospected. ABM gave them the logo. Person-level data gave them the deal.

**Tweet 4:**
B2B buying committees have 6-10 people. ABM targets 2-3 senior titles. The analyst doing the eval, the ops person who'll own the tool, the tech lead running integration tests. All invisible to ABM.
