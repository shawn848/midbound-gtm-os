**Tweet 1:**
100+ VC meetings in NYC, SF, LA. The real question in every room: Can this be absorbed? Can a bigger company build this in 3 months? If you can't answer with something concrete, the meeting ends politely.

**Tweet 2:**
Best advice I heard repeatedly: go vertical, win deeply in one segment, then expand. 10 customers who can't live without you > 100 who kind of like you. Sounds obvious. Incredibly hard to practice.

**Tweet 3:**
The test: would this customer be angry if you disappeared? Not inconvenienced. Angry. If yes, you've won deeply. If they'd shrug and find an alternative, you haven't.

**Tweet 4:**
Proprietary data is the moat that's hardest to replicate. Code can be rebuilt. Features copied. Speed is temporary. A dataset that improves with every customer and compounds over time? That's structural.

**Tweet 5:**
Velocity is a moat too. A team that ships 2x faster learns 2x faster and course-corrects 2x faster. Over 18 months that compounds into something a large company can't match.
