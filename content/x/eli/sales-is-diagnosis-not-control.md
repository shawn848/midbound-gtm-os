**Tweet 1:**
Imagine a doctor who asks one question, cuts you off 30 seconds in, and writes a prescription. You'd walk out. That's how most sales calls go. Prospect says a keyword. Rep dumps features for 20 min. Ghosted.

**Tweet 2:**
Good doctors create space for honest answers. In sales: shut up and listen. When prospects feel heard, they share the political dynamics, the competing priorities, the reason the last vendor failed. That's diagnostic gold.

**Tweet 3:**
Good doctors assume forward progress. Not "would you like a proposal?" but "I'll send a recommendation Thursday, we'll walk through it Friday." Confidence without pressure.

**Tweet 4:**
Good doctors lean into hard questions. "Is budget approved or exploratory?" "Who else weighs in?" Deals built on avoided questions collapse during procurement. Ask early.

**Tweet 5:**
Deals don't follow a selling process. They follow a buying process. Your job is diagnosis, not control. Understand where the buyer is and help them get to the next step.
