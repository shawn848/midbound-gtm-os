**Tweet 1:**
Company-level ID is a scam. "Someone from Google visited your pricing page." Google has 300K employees. Which one? You have nothing.

**Tweet 2:**
Your SDR gets the alert. Searches LinkedIn for 45 min. Picks a VP who looks right. Sends a cold email. That VP never visited your site. The actual visitor? An analyst running the evaluation. Nobody talks to them.

**Tweet 3:**
One of our customers surfaced an analyst during a POC. Nobody would have prospected that person. Turns out the analyst was leading the vendor evaluation. $30K closed deal.

**Tweet 4:**
Person-level identification gives you the actual human. Name, title, email, LinkedIn, pages viewed. In real time. Not "someone from Google." The specific person on your pricing page right now.

**Tweet 5:**
If you're spending real money on ads and getting 1K+ monthly visitors, you're leaving deals on the table every day. Stop paying for company logos. midbound.com
