We raised $70K from friends and family. Not VCs. People who trusted us more than our pitch deck justified. That money had names on it. It changes how you spend.

**Tweet 2:**
Built an auto-prospecting tool first. AISDR got commoditized overnight. Killed it. Started cold calling 10 founders a day. Same pain kept coming up: "We spend on ads and have no idea who's visiting."

**Tweet 3:**
Venezuela to Israel to a combat unit to startups. Comfort didn't make me. Pressure did. The period between killing product one and finding MidBound was the lowest point. But I'd been in worse spots.

**Tweet 4:**
MeetVisitors became MidBound. Six figures in revenue under 5 months. $0 CAC. Team of 3. Every customer from outbound, content, or word of mouth.

**Tweet 5:**
$70K from friends and family. Two dead products. Hundreds of cold calls. One product that works. I'd do every part again.
