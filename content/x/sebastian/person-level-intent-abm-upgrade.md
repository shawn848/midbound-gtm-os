Spending $50K/mo on ads. 10K visitors. Company-level tools give you 3,000 company names. Your team still has to guess WHO was browsing. That $50K funded a really expensive guessing game.

**Tweet 2:**
Person-level changes this. Someone hits your pricing page. Slack notification: "Sarah Kim, Head of Demand Gen at Notion, 4 min on pricing. Here's her email." Your SDR reaches out while she's still thinking about you.

**Tweet 3:**
3 people from the same account visit in one week? That's not a coincidence. That's a buying committee forming in real time. Multi-stakeholder detection, automatic.

**Tweet 4:**
A demand gen agency running $200K/mo for clients. Before: impression data. After: exact people their ads drove to the site. Agency retention doubled because they could prove ROI at the person level.
