**Tweet 1:**
$50M ARR company. ICP locked to VP+ only. Pipeline drying up. Then an analyst showed up on their pricing page. Nobody would have prospected them. That analyst closed a $30K deal.

**Tweet 2:**
The VP everyone was chasing had delegated the entire evaluation to the analyst. Months of SDR sequences on the wrong person. The actual buyer wasn't in the ICP.

**Tweet 3:**
Titles lie. An analyst running an evaluation committee has more influence than the exec who rubber-stamps the PO. Building your prospecting around titles means building on assumptions.

**Tweet 4:**
Your ICP should be a guide, not a gate. "We target VP+ at mid-market SaaS" is a strategy. "We ONLY talk to VP+" is a blindfold.

**Tweet 5:**
Pipeline doesn't die all at once. It dies in the margins. The analyst you filtered out. The mid-level manager you skipped. Open the box. See who's actually showing up.
