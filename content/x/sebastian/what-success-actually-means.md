**Tweet 1:**
Founder A: $10M raise, 3K LinkedIn likes, burning $800K/mo on $200K revenue. Founder B: bootstrapped, $3M ARR, 80% margins, team of 8. Nobody writes about Founder B. Who's more successful?

**Tweet 2:**
Revenue is vanity. Profit is sanity. $5M ARR sounds incredible until you learn the company spends $6M to generate it. That's a money transfer operation with a SaaS wrapper.

**Tweet 3:**
We hit six figures in 5 months. 3 people. $0 CAC. The startup world has this obsession with headcount and raise size as signs of progress. Spending more money faster is not progress.

**Tweet 4:**
Four metrics that actually matter: EBITDA (not revenue). Net revenue retention. Customer dependency (would they fight to keep you?). Cash relative to burn.

**Tweet 5:**
Are you building a business or building a story? Stories don't compound. Margins do. Retention does. Customers who depend on you do.
