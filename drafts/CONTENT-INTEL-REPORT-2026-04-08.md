## Executive Summary

Scraped **1,834 content items** across X/Twitter, LinkedIn, and Reddit from 5 thought leaders and 6 community sources. Built a SQLite database with 29-topic taxonomy. Identified **14 content ideas**, **6 competitive signals**, and **3 uncovered topic gaps** that no competitor is addressing.

**Key finding:** Vulnerability and revenue transparency content outperforms product content 10:1 across all platforms. Adam Robinson's revenue posts and Chris Walker's personal stories consistently get 5-17x more engagement than framework/product posts. MidBound has the raw material for this (real bootstrapping story, $0 CAC, 6 figures in 5 months) but hasn't deployed it yet.

## Pattern Analysis

### Adam Robinson (RB2B) -- Direct Competitor

**What works:**
- Revenue transparency: "$10M ARR is the FU money of SaaS" (97 likes, 14K views)
- Anti-VC takes: "VCs convincing founders to take money with 90%+ probability they fail" (157 likes)
- Category naming: Pushing "Contact-Based Marketing" (CBM) as the evolution beyond ABM
- Playbook content: "If I had zero customers and just signed up for RB2B, how I'd get to 100 in 90 days"

**Content strategy:**
- ~36 posts/month (roughly daily)
- Only 1 in 10 has a hard CTA
- Revenue numbers drive highest engagement
- "Prolific Zone" concept: authentic but not mainstream

**Positioning language:**
- "Person-level identification" (technical)
- "Contact-based marketing" (category name he's trying to own)
- "Company-level ID is an educated guess, not intelligence"

**Vulnerability to exploit:**
- RB2B free tier gutted early 2026 -- users actively looking for alternatives
- No content about AEO/GEO -- MidBound can own this intersection
- Robinson's content is self-referential (about HIM, not about buyer problems) -- MidBound can be more useful

### Chris Walker (Refine Labs / Passetto) -- Thought Leadership Model

**What works (insane engagement):**
- Personal vulnerability: "15 months ago I got fired" = 17,367 likes, 909 comments
- "I've been ghosted after a final interview" = 15,573 likes
- "A lot of people would look at my resume and think I'm average" = 10,162 likes
- Data-backed contrarian: "CONFIRMED WITH DATA: Paid social lead gen is terribly inefficient" = 3,655 likes
- Pipeline transparency: "Pipeline down 43% MoM" = 3,647 likes

**Frameworks MidBound should adopt:**
1. **Lead Gen vs. Demand Gen distinction** -- MidBound is demand CAPTURE (capturing existing intent), not lead GENERATION. No one in visitor ID has framed it this way.
2. **"25 things attribution misses"** -- Visitor intelligence fills every one of these gaps.
3. **"Worst performing posts drive the most revenue"** -- New thinking > popular recycled takes.
4. **Real numbers, always** -- Every pipeline claim backed by actual data from real companies.

### Guillermo Rauch (Vercel) -- Founder-as-Brand Model

**What works:**

- Growth transparency: "Vercel signups growing at 52% MoM" = 855 likes
- Customer listening: "We shape the product from X conversations" = 2,262 likes (his highest)
- Demo content: v0 moon flag simulation = 509 likes, 527K views

**Lessons for MidBound:**
- Customer stories > founder stories for a technical audience
- Growth metrics + conviction = credibility
- AI integration content resonates strongly right now
