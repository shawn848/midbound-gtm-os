## Competitive Positioning by Segment

### Against Snitcher (Most Common Comparison)
**Snitcher strength:** Simple, affordable, easy setup.
**The gap:** Company-level only. You see "Acme Corp visited" and then you're on your own to figure out who.
**MidBound pitch:** "Snitcher tells you the company. We tell you the person. Same setup simplicity, fundamentally different output."

### Against Factors (Second Most Common)
**Factors strength:** Strong analytics, multi-touch attribution, account scoring.
**The gap:** Account-level identification. Great analytics on which accounts engage, but no person resolution.
**MidBound pitch:** "Factors shows you which accounts are hot. We show you which people at those accounts are on your site. Use both if you want -- they solve different problems."

### Against Clearbit Reveal
**Clearbit strength:** Deep company data, tight HubSpot integration, well-known brand.
**The gap:** Reveal is company-level. Enrichment works on known contacts, not anonymous visitors.
**MidBound pitch:** "Clearbit enriches contacts you already know. MidBound identifies visitors you don't. The order matters."

### Against 6sense
**6sense strength:** Enterprise-grade intent data, predictive analytics, ABM orchestration.
**The gap:** Company-level identification. Expensive. Long implementation. Requires dedicated ops.
**MidBound pitch:** "6sense is powerful for enterprise ABM orchestration. For person-level visitor identification that deploys in minutes, that's MidBound."

### Against Demandbase
**Demandbase strength:** ABM ad targeting, account intelligence, enterprise integrations.
**The gap:** Same as 6sense -- company-level ID, enterprise pricing, heavy implementation.
**MidBound pitch:** Same as 6sense. Don't position as a replacement. Position as the person-level layer their ABM stack is missing.

## Displacement vs. Complement Positioning

| Competitor | Position MidBound As |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Snitcher | Displacement -- MidBound does everything Snitcher does, plus person-level |
| Factors | Complement or displacement depending on use case |
| Clearbit | Complement -- different problems (enrichment vs. identification) |
| 6sense | Complement -- MidBound adds person-level to their account-level |
| Demandbase | Complement -- same as 6sense |

## Talk Track Notes

- Always lead with what MidBound does, not what competitors don't do
- Name competitors directly -- being afraid to name them signals weakness
- Acknowledge their strengths honestly -- the buyer will respect it
- Focus on the capability gap (company vs. person), not product quality
- Never claim to know competitor match rates, pricing details, or customer specifics
