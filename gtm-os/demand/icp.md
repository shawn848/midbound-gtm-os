# Ideal Customer Profile

## Primary ICP: B2B SaaS Companies

### Firmographics
- **Company size:** 50-500 employees
- **Revenue:** $5M-$100M ARR
- **Model:** B2B SaaS with sales-assisted motion
- **ACV:** $3K+ (deal size justifies outreach cost)
- **Geography:** US-based or with significant US customer base

### Technographics
- Running paid acquisition (Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, or both)
- Using a CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive)
- Using marketing automation (HubSpot, Marketo, or Pardot)
- May already use company-level ID tools (Snitcher, Clearbit, 6sense)

### Behavioral Signals
- **Website traffic:** 1,000+ monthly US visitors (minimum threshold for meaningful identification volume)
- **Ad spend:** $5K+/month on paid acquisition (they care about ROI on traffic)
- **Content marketing:** Active blog or resource library (driving organic traffic)
- **ABM program:** Running account-based plays (MidBound upgrades their targeting)

### Pain Points
- Low form fill rates (sub 5% of visitors convert)
- "We don't know who's visiting our site"
- Ad spend with no visibility into who it attracts
- SDRs spending hours researching accounts before outreach
- ABM programs with account-level data but no person-level resolution

### Buyer Personas

**Primary: VP/Director of Marketing or Demand Gen**
- Owns the number. Needs to show pipeline from marketing spend.
- Frustrated by vanity metrics (traffic, impressions) without lead attribution.
- Currently using Google Analytics + maybe a company-level tool.

**Secondary: VP/Director of Sales or Revenue**
- Needs warm leads, not cold lists.
- Frustrated by MQL quality or SDR research time.
- Wants to know who's on the site before the outbound sequence.

**Tertiary: Marketing Ops / Revenue Ops**
- Evaluates tools. Manages integrations.
- Cares about data quality, CRM sync, workflow triggers.
- Will ask about accuracy, compliance, and implementation effort.

## Secondary ICP: Demand Gen Agencies

### Profile
- Digital marketing agencies serving B2B clients
- Running paid acquisition, content, and/or ABM programs
- Looking for differentiated services to offer clients
- 10-100 employees

### Why They Buy
- Add person-level visitor ID as a premium service to client engagements
- Prove campaign ROI with person-level attribution (not just anonymous sessions)
- Differentiate from agencies that only report on traffic and conversions
- Upsell existing clients with better data

## Disqualifiers

Do NOT target:
- B2C companies (consumer traffic doesn't match well)
- Companies with under 1,000 monthly US visitors (not enough volume)
- Pure self-serve products with no sales team (no one to act on identified visitors)
- Companies in heavily regulated industries where visitor identification may have compliance issues (healthcare, financial services) without explicit compliance guidance
