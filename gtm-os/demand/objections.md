# Objection Handling

## Objection 1: "Is this invasive? Isn't this creepy?"

**The concern:** People feel uncomfortable with the idea of being identified when they visit a website. This is the most common objection and the most important one to handle well.

**Eli's framing (use this):**
"It's not invasive; it's responsive. It's not cold; it's warm. You visited our site. You spent time on our pricing page. You clearly have some interest. Reaching out based on that behavior isn't stalking. It's good business. The alternative is we both pretend you didn't visit and you get a cold email in 3 months instead."

**Key points:**
- This is B2B context, not personal browsing
- The visitor demonstrated intent by visiting the site
- The alternative (cold outreach) is objectively worse for both parties
- MidBound identifies business professionals in a business context
- Acknowledge the concern genuinely. Never dismiss it.

**What NOT to say:**
- "It's fine, everyone does it" (dismissive)
- "It's no different from cookies" (inaccurate and reductive)
- "If they didn't want to be found, they shouldn't visit websites" (antagonistic)

## Objection 2: "How accurate is this? What's the match rate?"

**The concern:** Buyers want to know how many visitors MidBound actually identifies. They've been burned by tools that overclaim.

**Response framework:**
- Be honest: match rates vary by traffic profile
- B2B websites with US-heavy traffic and business-intent visitors see the best results
- Consumer traffic or international traffic will match at lower rates
- Every email surfaced is validated before delivery
- The 14-day free trial exists specifically so they can test on their own traffic

**What NOT to say:**
- A specific percentage (unless founders provide current, verified numbers)
- "We identify everyone" (false)
- "100% accuracy" (impossible)

## Objection 3: "We already use [Snitcher / Factors / Clearbit / 6sense]"

**The concern:** They already have a tool in the space. Why switch or add another?

**Response by competitor:**

**If using Snitcher:** "Snitcher gives you the company name. MidBound gives you the person. Try MidBound alongside Snitcher for 14 days and compare the output. If company-level is enough for your team, stay with Snitcher."

**If using Factors:** "Factors is strong on attribution and analytics. MidBound solves a different problem: identifying the individual visitor. Many teams use both."

**If using Clearbit:** "Clearbit enriches contacts you already know. MidBound identifies visitors you don't know yet. They're complementary."

**If using 6sense or Demandbase:** "Those are account-level orchestration platforms. MidBound adds person-level identification on top. Think of it as the missing layer."

**Key principle:** Position as complement or upgrade, not as a rip-and-replace (unless they're using Snitcher specifically for visitor ID).

## Objection 4: "This is too expensive / we don't have the budget"

**Response framework:**
- Reframe: what does it cost to NOT know who's visiting? Calculate wasted ad spend.
- "You're spending $X/month on ads. 97% of visitors leave anonymous. What's 10% of those identified visitors worth to your pipeline?"
- 14-day free trial, no credit card. Zero risk to test.
- Start the conversation with ROI math, not pricing.

**What NOT to say:**
- Specific pricing (unless founders provide current pricing publicly)
- "It pays for itself" without showing the math
- Discounting before proving value

## Objection 5: "We don't have enough traffic"

**The concern:** Their site doesn't get enough visitors to justify the tool.

**Response:**
- Minimum threshold: 1,000+ monthly US visitors for meaningful results
- If they're below that, MidBound may not be the right fit right now
- Suggest building traffic first (content, paid, SEO) and revisiting
- Be honest. Selling to someone without enough traffic hurts both parties.

## Objection 6: "Our sales team won't use it"

**The concern:** They've bought tools before that nobody adopted.

**Response:**
- Slack integration means reps see identified visitors in the tool they already live in
- HubSpot sync means contacts appear in the CRM automatically
- No new dashboard to learn. No new login. Data goes where they already work.
- Start with one rep on the Slack channel. Prove it with one converted lead.

## Objection 7: "Can you guarantee results?"

**Response:**
- No. And anyone who guarantees results is lying.
- What MidBound guarantees: you will see identified visitors with contact details. What your team does with that data determines results.
- The 14-day free trial is the proof. Test it on real traffic.

## General Objection-Handling Principles

1. **Acknowledge first.** Every objection is a legitimate concern until proven otherwise.
2. **Be honest.** If MidBound isn't the right fit, say so. Forcing a bad deal hurts long-term.
3. **Lead with the trial.** Most objections dissolve when they can test for free with zero commitment.
4. **Use math, not hype.** Calculate the cost of the status quo. Show the gap.
5. **Never dismiss privacy.** Use Eli's framing. Every time.
