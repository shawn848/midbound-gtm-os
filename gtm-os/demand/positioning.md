# Positioning

## Core Positioning Statement

**For** B2B SaaS companies and demand gen agencies **who** spend on marketing but have no visibility into who actually visits their website, **MidBound** is person-level website visitor identification **that** tells you exactly who visited -- name, title, email, LinkedIn profile -- not just which company. **Unlike** Snitcher, Factors, Clearbit, and other company-level tools, **MidBound** identifies the actual person, enabling warm outreach to known visitors instead of cold guessing.

## One-Liners

**Product:** Person-level website visitor identification for modern GTM.

**Problem:** 97% of your website visitors leave without converting. You have no idea who they are.

**Solution:** MidBound identifies your website visitors by name. Not the company. The person.

**Differentiator:** Every other tool tells you which company visited. We tell you which person.

## Positioning Layers

### Layer 1: What It Is
Person-level website visitor identification. AI matches website visitors to LinkedIn profiles in real-time. Output: name, title, company, validated email, LinkedIn profile, pages visited, time on site.

### Layer 2: Why It Matters
Because accounts don't buy. People do. Knowing "someone from Salesforce visited" is not actionable. Knowing "David Rodriguez, Director of Growth at Salesforce, spent 3 minutes on your pricing page" is.

### Layer 3: Who It's For
B2B companies with 1,000+ monthly US visitors, $3K+ ACV, and a sales team that acts on leads. Plus demand gen agencies who want to prove campaign ROI at the person level.

### Layer 4: Why Now
- Outbound is saturated. Cold emails get ignored. Warm outreach to known visitors converts.
- ABM platforms are expensive and still only do account-level. Person-level is the next evolution.
- Ad spend accountability is increasing. CMOs need to show who, not just how many.
- AI matching technology makes person-level identification viable at scale for the first time.

### Layer 5: Why MidBound
- Only person-level tool in the market (competitors are all company-level)
- 6 figures in under 5 months, $0 CAC, team of 3 (product-market fit is real)
- Deploys in minutes (vs. weeks for enterprise tools)
- Integrates with existing stack (Slack, HubSpot, webhooks)
- 14-day free trial, no credit card (low risk to try)

## Messaging Framework

### For Demand Gen Leaders
"You're spending $50K/month driving traffic. MidBound shows you exactly who that traffic is. Name, title, email. Not company names. People."

### For Sales Leaders
"Your reps are researching accounts that may or may not have visited your site. MidBound tells them exactly who visited and what they looked at. Turn your website into your best SDR."

### For ABM Teams
"Your ABM platform shows you which accounts are in-market. MidBound shows you which people at those accounts are on your site right now. Upgrade from account-level to person-level."

### For Agencies
"Your clients ask who their campaigns are reaching. Give them person-level answers, not anonymous session counts. MidBound makes you the agency with real attribution."

## Positioning Traps to Avoid

- **Do not position as a replacement for CRM or marketing automation.** MidBound feeds into those tools.
- **Do not position as a replacement for 6sense or Demandbase.** They do much more than identification. MidBound solves the identification gap.
- **Do not position as a surveillance tool.** It's business intelligence, not tracking.
- **Do not position on price alone.** The value is the capability, not the cost.
- **Do not overclaim on accuracy.** Match rates vary. Be honest about what MidBound can and cannot identify.
