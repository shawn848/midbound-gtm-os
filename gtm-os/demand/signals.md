# Intent Signals

## MidBound Signal Hierarchy

Not all website visits are equal. These signals, ranked by strength, indicate buying intent.

### Tier 1: Strong Intent (Act Immediately)

**Pricing page visit (2+ minutes)**
- The visitor is evaluating cost. They're past awareness and into consideration.
- Action: Slack alert to assigned rep. Same-day outreach.

**Multi-stakeholder visit (2+ people from same company in a week)**
- Multiple people from the same company evaluating your site signals an active buying committee.
- Action: Flag as active opportunity. Multi-thread outreach to each identified contact.

**Pricing page + integrations page in same session**
- They're checking if MidBound fits their stack. This is late-stage evaluation.
- Action: Priority Slack alert. Outreach referencing their integration interest.

**Return visitor to pricing page**
- Came back. Didn't sign up last time. Now they're back.
- Action: Immediate outreach. They're close.

### Tier 2: Medium Intent (Prioritize for Outreach)

**Case studies or testimonials page**
- Looking for social proof. In consideration phase.
- Action: Outreach with relevant case study or customer story.

**Blog post + pricing page in same session**
- Educated themselves on the problem, then checked the solution.
- Action: Outreach referencing the specific blog topic they read.

**Multiple pages in a single session (3+ pages, 5+ minutes)**
- Deep engagement. Not a bounce. Not a casual browse.
- Action: Add to outreach queue with page-visit context.

**UTM-tagged visit from a targeted campaign**
- They clicked your ad. They engaged with your content. Now they're on your site.
- Action: Outreach that references the campaign context.

### Tier 3: Awareness Signals (Nurture)

**Single blog post visit**
- Interested in the topic. Not necessarily interested in the product.
- Action: Add to nurture list. Send related content if they match ICP.

**Homepage visit only (under 2 minutes)**
- Awareness level. Might be referral traffic, might be research.
- Action: Score against ICP. Only outreach if high ICP fit and other signals present.

**Career page visit**
- Looking at jobs, not the product. Do not outreach.
- Exception: If the company is in your target account list, note the hire signal (expanding team = budget available).

## Signal Combinations

The strongest intent comes from signal stacking:

| Combination | Intent Level | Recommended Action |
|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Pricing + integrations + return visit | Very high | Immediate personal outreach |
| Multi-stakeholder + pricing page | Very high | Account-level play, multi-thread |
| Blog + pricing + 5+ min session | High | Same-day outreach with content context |
| UTM campaign + pricing page | High | Campaign-referenced outreach |
| Single page + high ICP score | Medium | Add to outreach queue |
| Single page + low ICP score | Low | Nurture or skip |

## ICP Score + Signal Matrix

Intent signals are more valuable on high-ICP visitors. A Director of Marketing from a 200-person SaaS company visiting your pricing page is a stronger signal than a student visiting the same page.

Combine ICP score (from visitor's firmographic and title match) with behavioral signals for a prioritized action list:
- **ICP 8-10 + Tier 1 signal** = Immediate action
- **ICP 8-10 + Tier 2 signal** = Same-day action
- **ICP 6-7 + Tier 1 signal** = Same-day action
- **ICP 6-7 + Tier 2 signal** = Outreach queue (24-48 hours)
- **ICP 5 or below** = Nurture only, regardless of signal
