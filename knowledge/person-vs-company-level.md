# Person-Level vs. Company-Level Identification

## The Core Problem

Most website visitor identification tools tell you which company visited your site. They do not tell you which person.

Knowing "someone from Salesforce visited your pricing page" is not actionable. Salesforce has 70,000+ employees. Who visited? What do they do? Are they a decision maker or an intern doing research? Company-level tools cannot answer this.

## Company-Level ID (What Competitors Do)

Tools like Snitcher, Factors, Clearbit Reveal, 6sense, and Demandbase resolve website visitors to company names using reverse IP lookup and similar techniques.

What you get:
- Company name
- Industry and company size
- Sometimes the pages visited

What you do NOT get:
- The person's name
- Their job title
- Their email address
- Their LinkedIn profile
- Whether they are a decision maker

The workflow with company-level ID:
1. See that "Acme Corp" visited your site
2. Guess who at Acme Corp it might have been
3. Search LinkedIn for people at Acme Corp who fit your ICP
4. Reach out cold to people who may or may not have been the actual visitor
5. Hope you picked the right person

This is educated guessing, not intelligence.

## Person-Level ID (What MidBound Does)

MidBound resolves website visitors to individual LinkedIn profiles using AI matching.

What you get:
- Full name
- Job title and seniority
- Company name
- Validated email address
- LinkedIn profile URL
- Pages visited, time on site, session behavior
- ICP fit score

The workflow with person-level ID:
1. See that "David Rodriguez, Director of Growth at Microsoft" visited your pricing page for 3 minutes
2. Check his ICP score -- high fit
3. Reach out with context: you know what he looked at, how long he spent, and exactly who he is
4. Your outreach is warm (he was already on your site), targeted (you know his role and company), and timely (you reached out the same day)

This is intelligence, not guessing.

## Why Person-Level Matters

### Speed to Lead
The fastest response wins. When you know the exact person within minutes of their visit, you can reach out the same day while your product is still on their mind. With company-level ID, you spend hours researching who to contact.

### Outreach Quality
"Hey David, noticed you were checking out our pricing -- happy to walk you through how teams like yours use MidBound" beats "Hey, saw someone from Microsoft visited our site" every time.

### Conversion Rates
Warm outreach to a known visitor converts at significantly higher rates than cold outreach to a guessed contact at a company that may have visited.

### Multi-Stakeholder Visibility
When three people from the same company visit your site, company-level tools show you one company. MidBound shows you three individuals, their roles, and what each one looked at. You can see the buying committee forming in real-time.

### Ad Spend Accountability
You can tie specific ad campaigns (via UTM) to specific identified individuals. Not "this campaign drove 50 company matches." Instead: "this campaign brought Director-level and above visitors from 12 target accounts, and 4 of them hit the pricing page."

## The Eli Framing

"Accounts don't buy. People do."

Company-level identification treats your website traffic like a corporate directory. Person-level identification treats it like a conversation already happening -- you just need to know who is talking.
