# Use Cases

## Primary Target Segments

### B2B SaaS Companies
**Profile:** Software companies selling to businesses, typically with sales-assisted motions and $3K+ ACV.

**The problem:** Running paid campaigns, content marketing, and SEO to drive website traffic. Converting 3% of visitors at best. The other 97% leave with no trace.

**How MidBound helps:**
- Identify the 97% who don't fill out a form
- Score visitors against ICP criteria
- Route high-fit visitors to sales for immediate outreach
- Track which marketing campaigns drive the highest-quality identified visitors
- See when target accounts are researching (multi-stakeholder detection)

**Example scenario:** A SaaS company spending $30K/month on Google Ads. Before MidBound: 2,000 clicks, 60 form fills. After MidBound: 2,000 clicks, 60 form fills + 400 identified visitors with contact details. The ad spend didn't change. The pipeline did.

### Demand Gen Agencies
**Profile:** Agencies running paid acquisition, content, and ABM programs for B2B clients.

**The problem:** Clients ask "who is visiting the site from our campaigns?" and the agency can only point to Google Analytics data -- anonymous sessions, not people.

**How MidBound helps:**
- Give clients person-level visibility into campaign-driven traffic
- Prove ROI by showing which campaigns drive which identified visitors
- Add MidBound as a value-add service to existing client engagements
- Differentiate from agencies that only report on anonymous traffic metrics

**Example scenario:** Agency running a LinkedIn Ads campaign for a client. Before: "Your campaign drove 500 visits to the landing page." After: "Your campaign drove 500 visits. Here are the 120 people we identified, sorted by ICP fit. 18 are Director-level or above at companies with 200+ employees."

### ABM Teams
**Profile:** Teams running account-based marketing programs targeting specific companies.

**The problem:** ABM relies on knowing which target accounts are engaging. Company-level tools help here. But knowing which people at those accounts are engaging is far more valuable.

**How MidBound helps:**
- See which individuals at target accounts visit the site
- Detect multi-stakeholder engagement (buying committee forming)
- Trigger account-specific plays when key personas visit
- Move from account-based to person-based marketing (ABM to PBM)

**Example scenario:** ABM team targeting 200 accounts. MidBound identifies that 3 people from Account #47 visited this week: the VP of Marketing, the Director of Demand Gen, and a Marketing Ops Manager. Each looked at the case studies page and pricing. This is a buying signal that company-level tools would show as "1 account visited."

## Key Use Cases

### Speed to Lead
Reach out to visitors the same day they visit your site. The faster the follow-up, the higher the conversion rate. MidBound identifies visitors in real-time and pushes alerts to Slack so reps can act immediately.

### Retargeting with Precision
Instead of retargeting all website visitors with the same generic ad, build segments based on identified visitor attributes. Retarget VP-level visitors with a different message than IC-level visitors. Retarget pricing page visitors differently from blog readers.

### Multi-Stakeholder Detection
When multiple people from the same company visit within a short window, it signals active evaluation. MidBound surfaces these patterns so sales can engage the full buying committee, not just one contact.

### UTM Re-Engagement
Track which campaigns (via UTM parameters) drive identified visitors. Re-engage visitors who came from specific campaigns with tailored follow-up. Tie ad spend directly to identified people, not just anonymous clicks.

### Pipeline Recovery
Deals that go dark often show up on your website before they re-engage. When a contact from a stalled deal visits your pricing page, MidBound alerts sales to re-engage at the right moment.

### Competitive Intelligence
When visitors from companies using competitor products visit your site, it signals active evaluation. MidBound identifies these visitors so sales can engage with displacement messaging.

## ICP Criteria for Best Fit

MidBound works best for companies that meet these criteria:
- **B2B model:** Selling to businesses, not consumers
- **1,000+ monthly US visitors:** Enough traffic to generate meaningful identification volume
- **$3K+ ACV:** Deal size justifies the cost of identification and outreach
- **Sales-assisted motion:** A team that acts on identified visitors (not pure self-serve)
- **50-500 employees:** Sweet spot for the current product
- **Running paid or content marketing:** Driving intentional traffic to the website
