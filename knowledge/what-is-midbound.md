# What Is MidBound

## One-Liner

MidBound does person-level website visitor identification. It tells you exactly who visited your site, not just what company they came from.

## The Output

When someone visits your website, MidBound tells you:

"David Rodriguez, Director of Growth at Microsoft, spent 3 minutes on your pricing page. Here's his validated email and LinkedIn profile."

Not "someone from Microsoft visited your site." The actual person. Their role. Their contact info. What they looked at. How long they stayed.

## The Thesis

97% of website visitors leave without converting. Most deanonymization tools tell you the company name and call it a day. But accounts don't buy. People do. MidBound identifies the people.

## How It Works (High Level)

MidBound uses AI to match website visitors to LinkedIn profiles in real-time. The matching happens against a proprietary dataset. Visitors get identified with:
- Full name
- Job title and company
- Validated email address
- LinkedIn profile URL
- Pages visited and time on site
- ICP fit score

Results are pushed to Slack, HubSpot, webhooks, or custom integrations in real-time.
