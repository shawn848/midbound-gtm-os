# Safety Filters -- MidBound

## Competitor Rules

**Safe to do:**
- Name competitors directly: Snitcher, Factors, Clearbit, 6sense, Demandbase
- Describe what they do accurately
- Highlight the company-level vs. person-level distinction
- Acknowledge competitor strengths where relevant

**Not safe to do:**
- Name specific competitor customers
- Claim to know competitor internal metrics, match rates, or churn
- Make up competitor weaknesses that are not publicly known
- Disparage competitor teams or individuals
- Claim MidBound fully replaces enterprise platforms (6sense, Demandbase do much more than just identification)

## Privacy and Compliance

MidBound identifies website visitors. This touches privacy sensitivities. Follow these rules:

**Never:**
- Claim MidBound is "GDPR compliant" or "CCPA compliant" unless explicitly confirmed by the founders with current legal review
- Make blanket statements about data privacy regulations
- Dismiss privacy concerns as irrelevant or overblown
- Use the word "tracking" loosely (MidBound identifies visitors, it does not track behavior across the internet)

**Always:**
- Handle the "is this invasive?" objection thoughtfully, using Eli's framing: "It's not invasive; it's responsive. It's not cold; it's warm."
- Acknowledge that privacy is a legitimate concern
- Position MidBound as identifying business visitors in a business context, not surveilling individuals
- Be precise about what MidBound does and does not collect

## Results and Claims

**Safe to say:**
- "6 figures in revenue in under 5 months" (founder-confirmed)
- "$0 customer acquisition cost" (founder-confirmed)
- "Team of 3" (founder-confirmed)
- "14-day free trial, no credit card required" (product fact)
- Fusion VC batch 16 (public information)

**Not safe to say:**
- Specific customer revenue impact numbers (unless provided by the customer for public use)
- "Guaranteed" anything -- identification rates, revenue lift, pipeline increase
- "100% accurate" identification
- Specific match rate percentages (unless founders provide current, verified numbers)

## Pattern vs. Person Test

Before publishing, verify:

**Safe to critique:**
- Company-level identification as an approach (the methodology, not the companies)
- Marketing practices that waste ad spend (generic patterns)
- Common GTM mistakes (industry-wide)
- The "old way" of doing ABM (not specific tools, but the approach)

**Not safe to critique:**
- Specific companies by name (except when comparing MidBound to named competitors, which is allowed in the competitive context)
- Individual practitioners or marketers
- Specific customers of competitors
- Partners or ecosystem companies

## Ecosystem Protection

| Category | Rule |
|----------|------|
| Integration partners (HubSpot, Slack) | Never critique. They are part of MidBound's value chain. |
| VC (Fusion) | Never critique. Positive or neutral only. |
| Competitor products | Critique the approach (company-level ID), not the teams. |
| Industry analysts | Do not position MidBound against analyst opinions. |
| Customers | Never name without explicit permission. Results should be directional, not specific. |

## Content Escalation

If a content request involves any of the following, flag for founder review before publishing:
- Naming a specific customer or their results
- Making compliance or legal claims
- Directly attacking a competitor's product quality (vs. comparing approaches)
- Referencing ongoing deals, partnerships, or fundraising
- Any content about pricing beyond "14-day free trial, no CC required"
