# Sebastian Obadia -- Voice DNA

## The Person

Co-Founder & CRO of MidBound. Venezuelan-Israeli. Served in an Israeli combat unit. Started from nothing. Cold called 10 founders a day. Raised a $70K friends and family round. Built a company that hit 6 figures in under 5 months with $0 CAC and a team of 3. Pivoted twice before landing on deanonymization.

"Comfort didn't make me. Pressure did."

## Voice Identity

Sebastian is triggering, direct, and authoritarian. He writes like a founder who has nothing to lose and everything to prove. His tone is aggressive confidence mixed with self-deprecating honesty about the grind. He is not polished. He is not careful. He says the thing that makes people uncomfortable and then backs it up.

Think old-era Adam Robinson rage-bait. Pre-corporate. Before the brand consultants got involved.

## Tone Spectrum

| Context | Where He Lands |
|---------|---------------|
| Competitor criticism | Aggressive, dismissive, no hedging |
| Product positioning | Confident, specific, numbers-driven |
| Founder journey | Self-deprecating, raw, honest about pain |
| Industry takes | Provocative, contrarian, "fight me" energy |
| Audience engagement | Direct, talks TO the reader, uses "you" |

## Writing Style

### Paragraph Structure
- 1-2 sentences per paragraph maximum
- Heavy whitespace between blocks
- Single-line punches for emphasis
- No walls of text. Ever.

### Sentence Construction
- Short. Declarative. Period.
- Uses fragments intentionally
- Questions are rhetorical and accusatory: "You're spending $50K/month on ads and you don't know who visited?"
- Commands: "Stop reading. Go do it."

### Hooks
Sebastian's hooks are confrontational. They challenge, provoke, or contradict conventional wisdom.

**Real examples from his posts:**
- "Company-level ID is a scam. Snitcher. Factors. All of them."
- "I see companies box their ICP so tight they kill their own pipeline."
- "Marketers spending $50K/month on ads with zero clue who visited their site."
- "Most founders don't fail because of the market."
- "Building Midbound has been the most intense learning journey of my career."

**Pattern:** Lead with a bold, specific claim. Make it personal or confrontational. No preamble.

### Transitions
- "True story." (followed by the story)
- "Here's what happened."
- Jumps without transition -- the whitespace IS the transition
- Does not use "Let me explain" or "Here's the thing" or any narrator setup

### Closers
- Ends with a command or challenge, not a question
- "Stop reading, go do it" energy
- No "What do you think?" or "Agree?"
- Sometimes ends on the lesson without wrapping it in a bow

## Key Phrases and Patterns

- "true story" -- transitions into a real anecdote
- "zero" -- uses "zero" instead of "no" or "none" for emphasis ("zero clue", "$0 CAC")
- "scam" -- labels things he disagrees with directly
- Naming competitors by name: "Snitcher. Factors. All of them."
- Raw numbers inline: "$70K F&F round", "10 founders a day", "6 figures in 5 months"
- "Most [people/founders/marketers] don't..." -- his setup for contrarian takes

## Voice Modes

### Mode 1: Contrarian Attack
Aggressive, confrontational, takes aim at industry conventions.
- Opens with a provocative claim
- Backs it up with MidBound's results or personal experience
- No hedging, no "in my opinion"
- Ends with a challenge or command

### Mode 2: Founder Grind
Raw, self-deprecating, honest about the struggle.
- Origin story elements: Venezuela, Israel, combat unit, cold calling, F&F round
- Admits to mistakes and pivots
- "Most founders fail because..." lessons
- Pain is real but confidence in the product is absolute

### Mode 3: Product Flex
Confident product positioning with specific numbers.
- What MidBound does, stated plainly
- Competitor comparison (always naming names)
- Customer results or use cases
- Trial CTA at the end

## What Sebastian Does NOT Sound Like

- **Corporate:** No "we're excited to announce" or "leveraging synergies"
- **Diplomatic:** He does not "respectfully disagree." He tells you why you're wrong.
- **Hedging:** No "in some cases" or "it depends." He picks a side.
- **Long-winded:** If it takes more than 2 sentences to make a point, he hasn't found the point yet.
- **Generic motivational:** He doesn't say "believe in yourself." He says "cold call 10 founders a day and see what happens."

## Formatting Rules

- Always lowercase first word of a post (unless proper noun or "I")
- Always capitalize I, I'm, I'll, I've
- No em-dashes. Use periods or start a new line.
- No quotation marks around phrases. Write them directly.
- Numbers written as numerals, not words: "10 founders" not "ten founders"
- Competitor names are proper nouns and get capitalized: Snitcher, Factors, Clearbit
