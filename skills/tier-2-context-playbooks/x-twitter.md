# X (Twitter) Playbook -- MidBound

> Inherits from: `tier-1-voice-dna/sebastian-voice.md` and `tier-1-voice-dna/eli-voice.md`

## Platform DNA

X is the compressed version of LinkedIn. Same voice per founder, faster pace, tighter constraints. LinkedIn is the essay. X is the headline.

## Tone

Same as LinkedIn but stripped to the bone. Every word earns its spot. No filler. No setup sentences.

- **Sebastian on X:** Even more aggressive. Single-line punches. Hot takes. Provocations.
- **Eli on X:** Tighter analogies. Revenue math in fewer words. Thesis statements that stand alone.

## Format Strategy

**Single posts (under 280 chars):** Hot takes, reactions, one-liners, product facts.

**Threads (4-7 tweets):** Condensed versions of LinkedIn posts. Each tweet stands alone -- someone scrolling should get value from any single tweet without reading the rest.

## Opening Line Style

- Always lowercase first word (same as LinkedIn), except I
- Even harder hooks than LinkedIn -- competing with the entire timeline
- First tweet of a thread must be self-contained

### Sebastian X Openers
- "company-level ID is dead. the tools just don't know it yet."
- "$50K/month on ads. zero idea who visited."
- "your website has more intel than your CRM. you're just not reading it."

### Eli X Openers
- "97% of your website visitors leave anonymous. that's 97% wasted."
- "accounts don't buy. people do."
- "you don't have a lead gen problem. you have a visibility problem."

## Character Awareness

- Single tweet: 280 characters. Write tight.
- Thread tweets: 280 each, but you have room across multiple
- Don't pad to fill 280. A 140-character banger beats a 280-character nothing.
- Line breaks count as characters. Use them sparingly on X.

## Paragraph Structure

- One sentence per line. Period.
- Single-line statements hit hardest
- No multi-sentence paragraphs on X
- Whitespace between thoughts (blank lines) only in threads

## Thread Format

```
tweet 1: hook (standalone value, stop the scroll)

tweet 2: context / the problem

tweet 3-4: the substance (how MidBound solves it, numbers, specifics)

tweet 5-6: example or analogy (compressed)

tweet 7: CTA or closer
```

Separate each tweet in drafts with `---` for easy copy-paste.

### Sebastian Thread Style
- Hook tweet is a provocation or bold claim
- Substance tweets are rapid-fire facts and numbers
- Closer is a command: "stop guessing. midbound.ai/register"
- 4-6 tweets max. Tight.

### Eli Thread Style
- Hook tweet is a reframe or thesis
- Substance tweets build the analogy or math
- Closer is "Prove me wrong." or a trial CTA
- 5-7 tweets. Slightly more room for the analogy to breathe.

## CTA Style

**Sebastian:**
- "stop guessing. midbound.ai/register"
- "14 days. free. no card."

**Eli:**
- "prove me wrong. or try it. midbound.ai/register"
- "14-day free trial. no credit card."

**Never:**
- "like and RT if you agree"
- "follow for more tips"
- "thoughts?"

## Cross-Posting Rules

| Content Type | Origin | X Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Contrarian takes | LinkedIn first | Condensed thread or single tweet 1-2 days later |
| Product education | LinkedIn first | Key stat or analogy as single tweet |
| Founder lessons | Either platform | X gets the punchline, LinkedIn gets the story |
| Hot takes | X native | Only cross-post to LinkedIn if it performs |

## Avoid

- Long paragraphs (this is not LinkedIn)
- Threads longer than 7 tweets
- Generic motivational content
- Engagement bait without substance
- Over-hashtagging (zero to one hashtag, zero is fine)
- Em-dashes, quotation marks, hype words
- Quotation marks around phrases
