# Content Pillar: Product Education

## Overview

Content that explains how MidBound works, what it does, and how teams use it. Not feature lists. Not marketing copy. Real explanations of what person-level identification means in practice, with specific use cases and scenarios.

## Primary Founder

Both founders, different angles:
- **Sebastian:** Aggressive product positioning. "Here's what it does. Here's why you need it. Stop guessing."
- **Eli:** Analytical explanation. Analogies, math, structured walkthroughs. "Here's how it works and here's what it means for your pipeline."

## Format

### Sebastian Product Education
1. **Problem opener** (1-2 lines): The pain point, stated aggressively. "You're spending $50K/month on ads and you don't know who visited your site."
2. **What MidBound does** (2-3 paragraphs): Specific features, stated plainly. Name, title, email, LinkedIn, pages visited, ICP score.
3. **Before vs. after** (2-3 paragraphs): What the workflow looked like without MidBound vs. with it.
4. **The closer** (1-2 lines): Command + CTA. "Stop guessing. midbound.ai/register"

### Eli Product Education
1. **Analogy opener** (1-2 paragraphs): A relatable scenario that maps to the product concept.
2. **How it works** (3-4 paragraphs): Structured explanation. AI matching, LinkedIn profile resolution, real-time delivery.
3. **What it means** (2-3 paragraphs): Business implications. Pipeline impact, speed to lead, ad spend accountability.
4. **The math** (1-2 paragraphs): "Here's the math:" followed by conversion numbers or ROI logic.
5. **CTA**: "Try it. 14-day free trial. midbound.ai/register"

## Product Education Topics

### Core Features
- **Person-level identification**: What it is, how it differs from company-level
- **Real-time delivery**: Visitors identified as they visit, not in batch reports
- **ICP scoring**: How visitors are scored against ideal customer criteria
- **Multi-stakeholder detection**: What it means when 3 people from the same company visit
- **UTM re-engagement**: Tying campaigns to identified visitors
- **Email validation**: Every surfaced email is validated before delivery

### Integrations
- **Slack alerts**: Real-time notifications when high-ICP visitors hit key pages
- **HubSpot sync**: Contacts created/updated automatically with visit data
- **Webhooks**: Raw data to any system (Clay, Zapier, custom backends)
- **Sequences**: Automated outreach triggered by visitor behavior

### Use Case Walkthroughs
- A SaaS company spending $30K/month on Google Ads: before and after MidBound
- A demand gen agency adding MidBound as a client deliverable
- An ABM team detecting buying committee formation at target accounts
- Speed-to-lead: reaching out within hours of a pricing page visit
- Pipeline recovery: catching dark deals that revisit your site

## What Makes This Pillar Work

- Concrete, not abstract. Specific features, specific scenarios, specific numbers.
- Shows the workflow, not just the capability. What does the sales rep actually do when a Slack alert fires?
- Addresses objections naturally within the education content (privacy, accuracy, pricing).
- Drives trial sign-ups by making the value obvious, not by hard selling.

## Safety

Low risk for most topics. Higher risk when discussing:
- Identification accuracy (never claim specific match rates unless founder-verified)
- Privacy (use Eli's framing, never dismiss the concern)
- Competitor comparisons (follow safety filters -- compare approaches, not teams)

Always reference `knowledge/` files for product details. Never fabricate features or capabilities.
