# Substance Requirements -- MidBound

## What Qualifies as Substance

### Insufficient (generic):
"Most B2B companies don't know who visits their website."

### Sufficient (substance):
"We talked to a SaaS company spending $30K/month on Google Ads. 2,000 clicks a month. 60 form fills. That's a 3% conversion rate, which they thought was fine. We ran MidBound on their traffic for a week. 400 identified visitors with full contact details. Same traffic, same spend. But now the sales team had 400 people to reach out to with context -- not 60 form fills and a lot of hope."

## The Test

Every substantive claim in MidBound content needs at least 2 of these 5:

- [ ] **Specific example with concrete details**: Numbers, tools, timeframes, scenarios. "$30K/month on ads" not "significant ad spend."
- [ ] **Technical specifics**: How the feature works, what the output looks like, what integrations are involved. "Slack alert fires with name, title, company, ICP score, and the page they visited" not "real-time notifications."
- [ ] **Reasoning shown**: Why something works or doesn't. Not just the conclusion, but the logic chain. "Company-level ID fails because knowing 'Acme Corp visited' doesn't tell you whether it was the VP of Marketing or an intern."
- [ ] **Consequences or results**: What happened? "6 figures in 5 months" or "sales went from 'we have no leads' to 'we have a prioritized list' in a week."
- [ ] **Gotchas or honest limitations**: What doesn't work perfectly. "Match rates vary by traffic profile. B2B sites with US-heavy traffic see the best results."

## Examples by Pillar

### Contrarian Takes -- Substance Required
**Weak:** "Company-level ID is outdated."
**Strong:** "Company-level ID tells you Acme Corp visited. Acme Corp has 2,000 employees. You're supposed to guess which one was on your pricing page? That's not intelligence. That's hope. MidBound showed us the Director of Growth from Acme Corp spent 3 minutes on the pricing page and then visited the integrations docs. That's a qualified lead with context."

### Product Education -- Substance Required
**Weak:** "MidBound integrates with your existing tools."
**Strong:** "When a visitor matching your ICP criteria hits your pricing page, a Slack notification fires in under a minute. Name, title, company, validated email, LinkedIn profile, the page they visited, and how long they stayed. Your rep sees it, clicks the LinkedIn link, and sends a connection request with context. The visitor is still thinking about your product when the request lands."

### Origin Stories -- Substance Required
**Weak:** "We worked hard and things eventually worked out."
**Strong:** "I cold called 10 founders a day for 3 months. Not emails. Calls. Most didn't pick up. The ones who did either hung up fast or gave me 90 seconds. I got better at those 90 seconds. The F&F round was $70K from people who knew me, not people who were impressed by a pitch deck."

### ABM Evolution -- Substance Required
**Weak:** "ABM needs to evolve to include person-level data."
**Strong:** "You have 200 target accounts. Your ABM platform says 40 are showing intent. Now what? You send your SDR team to research each one, find the right contact, write a personalized email, and hope they're the person who was actually on your site. With person-level ID, you skip straight to: 'David Rodriguez, Director of Growth at Account #47, spent 3 minutes on your pricing page yesterday.' The research is done. The signal is fresh."

### Founder Lessons -- Substance Required
**Weak:** "Pivoting is hard but sometimes necessary."
**Strong:** "We built auto-prospecting first. Shipped it. Got users. Kept hearing: 'This is cool but I need to know who's already visiting my site.' Pivoted to AISDR. Shipped it. Got users. Kept hearing the same thing. Third time: we built the thing they kept asking for. Deanonymization. 6 figures in 5 months. The market doesn't lie if you listen."

## When Substance Is Missing

If a draft fails the substance test (fewer than 2 of 5 criteria met on any substantive claim):

1. Identify which claims are unsupported
2. Add specific details from `knowledge/` files
3. If no specific details are available, restructure the claim as a question or hypothesis rather than a statement
4. Re-run the test

Do not publish content that fails the substance test. Generic B2B content actively harms MidBound's positioning.
